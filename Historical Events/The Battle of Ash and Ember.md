[[Hallowcrest]]’s origins are rooted in a pivotal moment in history. [[Ne're Darke]], a shadowy criminal organization with a presence across the plane, gained control of an Ancient Deep Dragon named [[Daurgothoth]] who inhabited the caverns of the towering mountain range in the recently discovered [[Silver Coast]]. Using psionic crystals mined from deep underground, [[Ne're Darke]] enslaved the dragon’s mind and unleashed terror on nearby regions.

The Sovereign Concord, alerted by their oracles, recognized the threat and launched a campaign to stop [[Ne're Darke]] forces. The battle culminated in the deployment of [[Gestalt Hallowbrand]], a legendary warrior, and his wyvern companion, [[Searclaw]], alongside an army of soldiers and adventurers. Among their ranks was [[Aurora Stellaris]], a brilliant mage tasked with destabilizing the psionic crystals and aiding the enslaved dragon’s resistance.

In a climactic confrontation in the center of what is now [[Hallowcrest]], [[Aurora Stellaris]] was able to destabilize the mass of psionic crystals for long enough so that Hallowbrand could slay [[Daurgothoth]], turning the tides of the battle and defeating [[Ne're Darke]] forces. Unfortunately, the cost was high, as [[Gestalt]] fell in the final battle while saving [[Aurora]] and his companions from an explosion caused by destroying the crystals. Grieving the loss of his master, [[Searclaw]] retreated to [[Spine of the North]], inhabiting a volcano, now known as [[Searclaw's Rest]], where he remains to this day.

In the aftermath of the battle, survivors established a settlement to honour the fallen and protect the region from future threats. Named [[Hallowcrest]], the city grew around the [[Twin Protectors]], a grand statue commemorating [[Gestalt Hallowbrand]] and [[Searclaw]]. Additionally, a large hill within the city was named [[Victory Hill]], where a memorial was established for the fallen soldiers. The city quickly became a beacon of hope and unity, attracting people from all walks of life.

[[Hallowcrest]] honours the sacrifices of the Battle of Ash and Ember with an annual festival, known as Victory Day, during which [[Searclaw]] flies over the city, his fiery silhouette a symbol of courage, strength, and renewal. The festival brings the city together in a vibrant celebration of its history and resilience.
