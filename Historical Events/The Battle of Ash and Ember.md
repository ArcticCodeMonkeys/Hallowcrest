[[Hallowcrest]]’s origins are rooted in a pivotal moment in history. In 1244 SE, [[Ne're Darke]] threatened to gain control of an ancient deep / black (accounts are inconsistent) dragon named [[Daurgothoth]] who inhabited the caverns of the towering mountain range in the recently discovered [[Silver Coast]]. Using psionic crystals mined from deep underground, [[Ne're Darke]] constructed [[The Mindrend Crown]] and enslaved the dragon’s mind and unleashed terror on nearby regions.

The Sovereign Concord, alerted by their scouts in [[Crackrock Quarry]], recognized the threat and launched a campaign to stop [[Ne're Darke]] forces. The battle culminated in the deployment of [[Gestalt Hallowbrand]], a legendary warrior, and his draconic companion, [[Searclaw]], alongside an army of soldiers and adventurers. Among their ranks was [[Aurora Stellaris]], a brilliant mage tasked with destabilizing the psionic crystals and aiding the enslaved dragon’s resistance.

The battle was split into three main conflicts, the first and longest took place in [[Crackrock Quarry]]. The quarry was the first place to spot [[Ne're Darke]] members and became an intense battlefield. The winding tunnels became a filled with blood and bodies, and Ne're Darke had an extreme advanntage fighting in the dark. [[Gestalt Hallowbrand]] made the decision to fall back after loosing many soldiers to intentionally move the battlefield above ground and abandon [[Crackrock Quarry]], which then became [[The Crypts]].

Unfortunately, this decision meant that [[Ne're Darke]] controlled the region where [[Daurgothoth]] was located, and though there was no easy path to the peak, in a few days time their forces would reach the top. The frontlines were stuck at a stand still, and so [[Gestalt Hallowbrand]] devised a plan to quickly be able to break through. He and [[Searclaw]] were an easy target to artillery, so he had to shake up the front lines and force them to retreat. He ordered over 2000 barrels of Smokepowder to be delivered from [[The Capital]], stored in a main encampment which is now [[Smokepowder Keep]]. The barrels were buried underground under loose dirt along the frontlines, and [[Gestalt Hallowbrand]] rode [[Searclaw]] into the sky, commanding his forces to retreat. As the [[Ne're Darke]] soldiers advanced through the empty space, they walked over the Smokepowder line, and [[Searclaw]]'s fiery breath ignited the row of barrels, decimating their forces and destroying their artillery. This crater is now the river / canal known as [[The Vive]].

Finally, [[Gestalt Hallowbrand]], [[Searclaw]], and [[Aurora Stellaris]] were flew up to the peak of the mountain, an inactive volcano. [[Ne're D. Darke]] had already reached the peak, and placed [[The Mindrend Crown]] on [[Daurgothoth]], placing him under his control. In a climactic battle, [[Daurgothoth]] gravely wounded [[Searclaw]], cracking his horn off and creating the powerful artifact known as [[The Ashen Horn]]. [[Aurora Stellaris]] was able to disrupt the psionic crystals for long enough, allowing [[Gestalt Hallowbrand]] to slay [[Daurgothoth]], but unfortunately [[Searclaw]] was still reeling from his wounds, and [[Ne're D. Darke]] was able to get the jump on [[Gestalt Hallowbrand]], killing him. In a fit of rage, [[Searclaw]] let out a mighty breath of fire, setting off the volcano, and killing [[Ne're D. Darke]].

[[Aurora Stellaris]] managed to escape with  [[The Ashen Horn]], as did [[Shadow]], [[Ne're D. Darke]]'s [[Soul Bond]] companion. [[Searclaw]] remained within the volcano, mourning the loss of [[Gestalt Hallowbrand]] and the mountain was dubbed [[Searclaw's Rest]]. The volcano's spew decimated remaining [[Ne're Darke]]'s army who were forced to retreat back to the [[Underdark]]. [[The Mindrend Crown]] was lost, while [[Aurora Stellaris]] says it is indestructible, she was unable to find it even after years of divination.

In the aftermath of the battle, [[Aurora Stellaris]] established and lead a settlement to honour the fallen and protect the region from future threats. Named [[Hallowcrest]], the city grew around the [[Twin Protectors]], a grand statue commemorating [[Gestalt Hallowbrand]] and [[Searclaw]]. Additionally, a large hill within the city was named [[Victory Hill]], where a memorial was established for the fallen soldiers. The city quickly became a beacon of hope and unity, attracting people from all walks of life. 

[[Hallowcrest]] honours the sacrifices of the Battle of Ash and Ember with an annual festival, known as [[Victory Day]], during which [[Searclaw]] flies over the city, his fiery silhouette a symbol of courage, strength, and renewal. The festival brings the city together in a vibrant celebration of its history and resilience.
