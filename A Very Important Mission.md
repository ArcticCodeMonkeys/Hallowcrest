
[[Ember's Creed]] needs to send 6 large crates of Ale up to the [[Scorched Peak]] for the monk's celebrations. The journey is arduous and takes a grueling 22 hours. Checks throughout the way against the weather, slipping, and climbing tough areas. Failing them results in loosing ale and wine and leads to less payout. One combat encounter against a pack of wolves along the way.

Reward: 5 or more crates: 1 Renown for Ember's Creed
