*Uncommon, Spellcasting Focus (200gp), (Requires Attunement by a Bard)*

This list of jokes has been ‘passed on’ from bard to bard, eventually ending up in your hands. There are some pieces of comedy gold in here, some amazing insults… but mostly just ramblings.

**Personal Insult:** You ‘borrow’ a funny insult from the list, hurts for them though… You learn the Vicious Mockery cantrip. Whenever you deal damage with Vicious Mockery, roll the damage die twice and choose the highest. On a critical failure, deal both instances of damage.

**An Oldie:** You steal a classic joke that is sure to get a laugh. You learn Tasha’s Hideous Laughter and can cast it once per short rest without expending a spell slot. You can cast Vicious Mockery as a bonus action when targeting a creature under the effects of your Tasha’s Hideous Laughter spell.

**Crickets…** (Mastery: Cast Tasha’s Hideous Laughter 7 Times): You tell a horrible joke, so bad that no-one says anything, not even a cough. Once per long rest, you can cast Silence without expending a spell slot. When you cast Silence this way, the only verbal component that can be provided within the spell's area is the verbal component for either Vicious Mockery or Tasha’s Hideous Laughter.
