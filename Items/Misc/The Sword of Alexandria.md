
Spellcasting Focus (Greatsword), Very Rare (Requires Attunement)

*(Requires: Score a critical hit with a Spiritual Weapon against a creature of significant threat; conduct a ritual expending a diamond worth 1000 gold)*

While holding this sword, you can cast spells through it as it were a +1 spell casting focus. While attuned to the sword, you are considered proficient with it and can use it to make attacks. When you do, you can use your Wisdom modifier in place of Strength for attack and damage rolls.

Once per half rest, you can cast Spiritual Weapon from the sword without expending a spell slot. When you do, the sword floats out of your hand and you cannot use it to attack or cast spells. For the duration, your Spiritual Weapon can move up to 30 feet whenever you command it to move. Whenever the spell ends, the sword returns to your hand and regains its above properties.
