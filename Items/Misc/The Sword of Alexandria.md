
Spellcasting Focus (Greatsword), Very Rare (Requires Attunement)

*(Requires: Score a critical hit with a Spiritual Weapon against a creature of significant threat; conduct a ritual expending 1000 gold worth of holy water)*

While holding this sword, you can cast spells through it as it were a +1 spell casting focus. While attuned to the sword, you are considered proficient with it and can use it to make attacks. When you do, you can use your Wisdom modifier in place of Strength for attack and damage rolls.

**Guided Blade.** Once per half rest, you can cast Spiritual Weapon from the sword without expending a spell slot. When you do, the sword floats out of your hand and you cannot use it to attack or cast spells. For the duration, your Spiritual Weapon can move up to 30 feet whenever you command it to move. Whenever the spell ends, the sword returns to your hand and regains its above properties.

*(Requires: Score a critical hit with the Sword of Alexandria using the Attack action and as a Spiritual Weapon against a creature of significant threat; conduct a ritual expending a ruby worth 5000 gold*

**Guardian Angel.** The Sword of Alexandria grants +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with it. Additionally, all allied creatures within 5 feet of a Spiritual Weapon you conjure gain a +2 bonus to AC.

*(Requires: Score a critical hit with the Sword of Alexandria (in any manner) against a creature of significant threat and end the Dying condition on an ally on the same turn; conduct a ritual expending a diamond worth 10,000 gold)*

**Alexandria's Might** The Sword of Alexandria grants a +2 bonus to Spell Save DC and Spell Attack Rolls and the following additional benefits.

- You can use Guided Blade at will. 
- Whenever you cast Spiritual Weapon, it does not require concentration. The spell still ends if you are incapacitated or unconscious.
- Whenever you take the Attack action using The Sword of Alexandria, you may make an additional attack as a part of that action.



*(Requires: Score a critical hit with a Spiritual Weapon against a creature of significant threat; conduct a ritual expending 1000 gold worth of holy water)*

The Sword of Alexandria is treated as both a Spellcasting Focus and a Greatsword. While holding and attuned to the Sword of Alexandria, you can channel its magic in different ways. At the start of each of your turns, choose an art below to gain its benefits.

**Heavenly Strikes.** The Sword gains a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls. You are considered proficient with the sword and ignore the Heavy property. You can use Wisdom in place of Strength for attack and damage rolls made with the sword.

**Unyielding Faith.** The Sword gains a +1 bonus to Spell Save DC and Spell Attack Rolls. Whenever you cast Spiritual Weapon through the sword, the distance it can move increases to 40 feet.

**Guided Blade.** Once per half rest, you can cast Spiritual Weapon without expending a spell slot. When you do, the sword floats out of your hand and becomes the Spiritual Weapon. For the duration, you can cast spells through The Sword of Alexandria as if you were wielding it. When you do, the spell is cast as if you were in its space.

*(Requires: Score a critical hit with the Sword of Alexandria using the Attack action and as a Spiritual Weapon against a creature of significant threat; conduct a ritual expending a ruby worth 5000 gold)*

The Sword of Alexandria bonds with you further, increasing its bonus in Heavenly Strikes and Unyielding Faith to +2. 

**Guardian Angel.** All allied creatures within 5 feet of a Spiritual Weapon you conjure gain a +2 bonus to AC.

*(Requires: Score a critical hit with the Sword of Alexandria (in any manner) against a creature of significant threat and end the Dying condition on an ally on the same turn; conduct a ritual expending a diamond worth 10,000 gold)*

**Mastered Blade.** The Sword of Alexandria has both Heavenly Strikes and Unyielding Faith at all times.
