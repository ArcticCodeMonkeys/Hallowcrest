(Common, Wondrous) (Consumable) (10gp)

A delicious sandwich sold by [[Jim's Sandwich Shop]] which triggers wild magical effects when consumed.

A creature can consume this sandwich as a bonus action, triggering a Wild Magic Surge. When the effect of the Wild Magic Surge ends, the creature must succeed a DC 10 Constitution Saving Throw or gain a level of exhaustion. The DC is increased by 2 for each of these sandwiches a creature has consumed within the past 24 hours.
