*Rapier, Rare, (3000gp), (Requires Attunement)*

The blade of this rapier is a finely cut and beautiful verdant emerald and its hilt a polished platinum. It slices through the air with grace and is perfectly balanced. You have a +2 bonus to attack rolls made with this magic weapon. Additionally, whenever you wield this weapon in one hand with nothing in the other, you unlock the following effects:

- **Suave Swordplay:** You can add your charisma modifier to damage rolls made with this weapon. Additionally, you can add your charisma modifier to initiative rolls in place of your dexterity modifier.

- **Sword Dance:** You are under the effects of the [Kinetic Jaunt](https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/spell:kinetic-jaunt) spell.

- **Glint of Avarice:** Once per long rest, you can cast [Incite Greed](https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/spell:incite-greed) (*DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Charisma Modifier)* through the sword, using it as a replacement for the material and somatic components.
