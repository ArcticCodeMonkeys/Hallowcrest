*Rare, Dagger, (4000gp), (Requires Minor Attunement)*

This syringe functions as a +2 Dagger with the following additional benefits.

**Poison Vial.** Injury poisons you apply to this weapon retain their potency and do not dry out. You can hold only one poison at a time on this weapon.

**Inject.** Injury poisons applied with this weapon deal an additional 2 dice of damage, increase their DC by 2, and last twice as long. On a melee hit, you can choose to only inject half of the poison, losing the benefits of this feature until you reapply poison to this weapon. When you do, the poison remains on this weapon for one more use.
