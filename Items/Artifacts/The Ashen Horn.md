Once part of [[Searclaw]], his horn broke off in [[The Battle of Ash and Ember]], creating this artifact. It can be blown in to call for any nearby dragons to aid in whatever to user wishes. The Dragon can attempt to resist, but cannot always do so. Currently within the [[Sealed Vault]]


Wondrous Item, Legendary

As a Magic Action, you can blow into the Ashen Horn and play one of three notes, controlled by which mouth piece you blow into. 

**Call to Battle.** All Dragons within 100 miles hear the call of the Ashen Horn and are drawn towards you with the goal of destruction. All creatures within 1 mile must succeed a DC 20 Wisdom Saving Throw or be overcome with an urge for battle for the next 24 hours. On a failed save, a creature has advantage on attack rolls and gains the Tavern Brawler or Martial Weapon Training feat, and is compelled to attack the nearest creature to it at all times. If the target sees a creature it would normally consider an enemy, it prioritizes fighting that creature instead of the nearest one. This effect only ends if dispelled, or if the creature is reduced to 0 hit points.
