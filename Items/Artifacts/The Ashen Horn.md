![[Ashen Horn.jpg]]

Once part of [[Searclaw]], his horn broke off in [[The Battle of Ash and Ember]], creating this artifact. It can be blown in to call for any nearby dragons to battle, protect, or rest. Dragons and other creatures can all be affected, but dragons cannot resist its call. Currently stolen and missing.

%%CENSOR%%
Wondrous Item, Legendary

As a Magic Action, you can blow into the Ashen Horn and play one of three notes, controlled by which mouth piece you blow into. 

**Call to Battle.** All Dragons within 100 miles hear the call of the Ashen Horn and are drawn towards you with the goal of destruction. All creatures within 1 mile must succeed a DC 20 Wisdom Saving Throw or be overcome with an urge for battle for the next 24 hours. On a failed save, a creature has advantage on attack rolls and gains the Tavern Brawler or Martial Weapon Training feat, and is compelled to attack the nearest creature to it at all times. If the target sees a creature it would normally consider an enemy, it prioritizes fighting that creature instead of the nearest one. This effect only ends if dispelled, or if the creature is reduced to 0 hit points.

**Call to Protect.** All Dragons within 100 miles hear the call of the Ashen Horn and are drawn towards you with the goal of protection. All creatures within 1 mile must succeed a DC 20 Wisdom Saving Throw or be overcome with an urge to protect for the next 24 hours. On a failed save, a creature gains a +2 bonus to AC and Saving Throws and cannot willingly be more than 10 feet away from the person(s) or object(s) they value the most. If a creature is sworn to an oath to protect, such as a member of the [[Sovereign Guard]], they instead must defend that which they swore to protect.

**Call to Rest.** All creatures within 1 mile must succeed a DC 20 Wisdom Saving Throw or be overcome with an urge to put down their weapons for the next 24 hours. Dragons automatically fail this saving throw and cannot use a Legendary Resistance to overcome it. On a failed save, a creature is compelled to no longer fight unless necessary for their survival, regardless of their goals or ideals. %%/CENSOR%%
