A powerful crown used to subjugate the minds and will of creatures. Works better on creatures possessing or bearing a Psionic Crystal. Lost in [[The Battle of Ash and Ember]] and current location is unknown.

Legendary Circlet, (Requires Attunement)

While attuned to the Mindrend Crown, you are immune to the effects of Enchantment spells as well as the Charmed and Frightened conditions. The Mindrend Crown has 20 charges, which it can use for the following effects.

**Enslave (8 Charges).** As a Magic Action, you attempt to completely obliterate the mind of a creature you can see within 60 feet. The creature must make a DC 19 Intelligence Saving Throw or be placed under the effects of Dominate Monster. Creatures with an Intelligence of 6 or lower automatically fail this save. The effect only ends if dispelled, when you designate it, when you remove the Mindrend Crown, or when you enslave more creatures than your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma modifier (whichever is higher). 

**Mindrending Magic.** While holding this staff, you can use an action to expend 1 or more of its charges to cast one of the following spells from it, using your spell save DC and spell attack bonus or 19 and +13, whichever is higher: Tranfix (7 charges), Mind Blank (8 charges), Compelled Duel (1 charge), Fear (3 charges), Compulsion (4 charges), Geas (5 charges), Command (1 charge), Modify Memory (4 charges), or Hold Monster (5 charges).

**Commanding Subjugation.** Creatures have disadvantage on saving throws they make against being Charmed or Frightened by you. Additionally, the casting time for the Command spell becomes a Bonus Action for you.
