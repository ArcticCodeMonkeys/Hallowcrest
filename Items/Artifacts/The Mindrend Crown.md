A powerful crown used to subjugate the minds and will of creatures. Works better on creatures possessing or bearing a Psionic Crystal. Lost in [[The Battle of Ash and Ember]] and current location is unknown.

Legendary Circlet, (Requires Attunement)

While attuned to the Mindrend Crown, you are immune to the effects of Enchantment spells as well as the Charmed and Frightened conditions. The Mindrend Crown has 20 charges, which it can use for the following effects.

**Enslave (8 Charges).** As a Magic Action, you attempt to completely obliterate the mind of a creature you can see within 60 feet. The creature must make a DC 19 Intelligence Saving Throw or be Enslaved. Creatures with an Intelligence of 6 or lower automatically fail this save. While Enslaved, you can issue a command to the creature at the start of each of your turns which it must follow to the best of its abilities. The Enslaved condition only ends when you designate it, when you remove the Mindrend Crown, or when you enslave more creatures than your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma modifier (whichever is higher).
