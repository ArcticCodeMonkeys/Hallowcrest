A manifestation of a [[Precursors]] mind placed within a magical orb. It is capable of advanced divination, prediction, and strategy. It knows everything that is public information and can be queried for information and predictions about the future. Recently discovered in a [[Precursors]] ruin in the [[Spine of the North]], being brought to [[Hallowcrest]] by [[The Party]] to be researched by [[Factions/Aurora College|Aurora College]].
