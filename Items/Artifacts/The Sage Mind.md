%%CENSOR%%A manifestation of a [[Precursors]] mind placed within%%/CENSOR%% a magical orb. %%CENSOR%%It is capable of advanced divination, prediction, and strategy. It knows everything that is public information and can be queried for information and predictions about the future. Recently discovered in a [[Precursors]] ruin in the [[Spine of the North]],%%/CENSOR%% , brought to [[Hallowcrest]] by [[The Party]] to be researched by [[Factions/Aurora College|Aurora College]]. Stolen by [[Quinn Hammer]] and currently in possession of [[The Royale Flush]].

%%CENSOR%%See Also: [[People/Precursors/The Sage Mind|The Sage Mind]] (Personification)%%/CENSOR%%


%%CENSOR%%
Legendary Spell Casting Focus, (Requires Attunement)

While attuned to the Sage Mind, you must share your thoughts and vision with the Sage within. It shares your senses and awareness, while heightening your mental and visuospatial capabilities.

**Dual Mind.** You can concentrate on two spells provided at least one of them was cast through the Sage Mind. When you are forced to make a concentration saving throw, you roll one for each spell you are concentrating on.

**Astute Observation.** You gain proficiency in Insight, Perception, and Investigation. If you already have proficiency in any of these skills, you instead gain Expertise with that skill.

**All Knowing.** Any roll below a 15 on an Intelligence based skill check is treated as a 15.

**Spellcasting.** The Sage Mind has 20 charges which can be used to cast its spells. While holding the Sage Mind, you can take the Magic Action to expend 1 or more of its charges to cast one of the following spells from it, using your spell save DC and spell attack bonus: Scrying (at will), Sending (at will), [_globe of invulnerability_](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Spells:globe%20of%20invulnerability?expansion=34047#content) (6 charges), [_hold monster_](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Spells:hold%20monster?expansion=34047#content) (5 charges), _[levitate](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Spells:levitate?expansion=34047#content)_ (2 charges), [_lightning bolt_](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Spells:lightning%20bolt?expansion=34047#content) (5th-level version, 5 charges), [_magic missile_](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Spells:magic%20missile?expansion=34047#content) (1 charge), [_ray of enfeeblement_](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Spells:ray%20of%20enfeeblement?expansion=34047#content) (1 charge), or Foresight (16 charges)
