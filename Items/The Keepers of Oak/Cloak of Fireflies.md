
Rare, Cloak (1500gp) (Requires Attunement by a Druid or Ranger)

This cloak, composed of a light cloth covered in swarming fireflies, emanates dim light in a 10ft radius around it. While attuned to the cloak, you can cast each of the following spells for free once per long rest: Light, Faerie Fire, Blur, Hypnotic Pattern. Additionally, whenever you take damage, you can expend a use of one of these spells to disappear in a flash of light, becoming invisible until the start of your next turn.
