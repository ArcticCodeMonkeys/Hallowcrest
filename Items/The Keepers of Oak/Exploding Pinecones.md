
*Common, Consumable (30gp)*

![[Exploding Pinecone.jpg]]

These pinecones come from a peculiar tree which, when exposed to direct sunlight or moderate heat, causes them to drop and explode, spreading their seeds everywhere. As an action, you can light and throw this pinecone to a point within 20 feet. All creatures within 10 feet must succeed a DC 13 Dexterity Saving Throw or take 2d6 piercing damage.
