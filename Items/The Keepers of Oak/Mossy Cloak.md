Uncommon, Cloak (300gp) (Requires Attunement)

This cloak is composed of a thick and resilient moss, similar to that of a Shambling Mound.

**Natural Protection:** While wearing this cloak, you have advantage on saving throws against harsh conditions and survival checks made to stay on track.

**Grasping Vines:**

While wearing this cloak, you can cast Thorn Whip at will, and Ensnaring Strike or Entangle once per short rest (+5, DC 13)

**Shambling Absorption:** Whenever you take lightning damage, reduce the damage taken by 1d8.
