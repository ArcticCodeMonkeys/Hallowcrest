Rare Robes (Attunement)

While wearing these robes, you have a +1 bonus to Dexterity and Strength saving throws. You also gain a swimming speed equal to your walking speed. 

**Surging Tide**: As an action, you can send a surge of water in a 20 foot wide, 10 foot long line. All creatures in the area must succeed a DC 15 Strength Saving Throw or be knocked prone, be pushed back 15 feet, and take 3d6 bludgeoning damage. On a success the target only takes half damage.

**Whirlpool**: As an action, you can conjure a whirlpool of waves in a 15 foot emanation. All hostile creatures treat this area as difficult terrain and take 2d8 cold damage whenever they start their turns in the area or enter it for the first time on their turns. As a bonus action, you can push any creatures within the area 15 feet in a direction of your choosing which must be away from you. This effect lasts 1 minute or until you are knocked prone, grappled, restrained or incapacitated.

**Riptide**: As an action, you can conjure a powerful flow of the tides to pull one creature within 60 feet. The target must succeed a DC 15 Strength Saving Throw or be pulled up to 60 feet towards you and until the end of its next turn, the target is vulnerable to cold damage and cannot move.

Once you use one of these features, you cannot do so again until you complete a short rest.
