Uncommon, Dagger(s) (Minor Attunement)

These daggers have a +1 bonus to hit and damage rolls. Additionally, they have 5 charges which recharge on a long rest which you can use for the following effects.

**Venom Strike.** When you hit with an attack, you can expend 1 charge to deal an additional 1d4 poison damage. You can expend an additional charge to force the target to make a DC 14 Constitution Saving Throw or be poisoned until the end of its next turn.

**Poison Spittle.** In place of a weapon attack, you can expend 2 charges to shoot poison from the daggers at a creature you can see within 30 feet, making a thrown weapon attack. On a hit, the target takes 2d8 poison damage and is blinded until the start of your next turn.
