
Rare, Scroll (1000gp)

A simple scroll detailing a complex tax form. As an action, you can read it aloud to a creature which can hear you within 60 feet. That creature must succeed a DC 15 Intelligence Saving Throw or choose one of the negative effects from the following options:

-          A random ability score is reduced by 4

-          Your AC is reduced by 2

-          Your hit point total and maximum is reduced by 3 times your level or CR

These effects last up for 1 minute. Alternatively, the creature expend 2d4 x 10gp to automatically succeed on the saving throw, but have their movement speed reduced to 0 until the end of their next turn.
