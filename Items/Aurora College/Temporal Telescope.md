Wand Rare (Requires Attunement)

While attuned to this Telescope, you gain proficiency in Wisdom (Perception) and (Insight). You can use the Telescope as if it were a Spyglass. 

Additionally, the Temporal Telescope has 7 charges, which recharge daily at midnight. The charges can be used to cast the following spells: Guiding Bolt (2), Augury (3), See Invisibility (3), Astral Flood (5), Divination (7). The Spell Save DC is 15 and the Spell Attack Modifier is +7.
