Wonderous Item (Rare) (Minor Attunement)

Inscriptions of a Solemn Oath written upon a piece of armor, a weapon, or a religious symbol. Whenever you fail a Wisdom saving throw, you can repeat the saving throw. On a success, your next attack has advantage and deals an additional 1d8 radiant damage. Once you use this feature, you cannot do so again until you finish a short rest.
