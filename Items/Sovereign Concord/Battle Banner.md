*Rare, Wonderous Item, (4000gp), (Requires Minor Attunement)*

As an action, you can plant this banner into the ground, granting you and your allies in a 30 foot emanation from it the following benefits for the next minute, or until the banner is removed.

**Inspired.** All creatures in the emanation gain a Heroic Inspiration. If they already have one, they can immediately roll 1d20 and gain temporary hit points equal to the amount rolled instead.

**For the Cause.** All members of the Sovereign Concord have advantage on Concentration Saving Throws and Saving Throws against the Charmed and Frightened conditions while within the emanation.

**Charge into Battle.** Any creature who starts their turn in the emanation with temporary hit points gains a +10 foot bonus to their movement speed until the temporary hit points expire or until the emanation disappears, whichever comes first.
