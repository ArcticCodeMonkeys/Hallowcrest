Very Rare (Great Sword)
(Requires Attunement)

This silver sword, decorated with intricate designs and patterns resembling the scales and wings of a dragon, once belonged to the famous [[Gestalt Hallowbrand]]. The blade is incredibly sharp and slightly warm to the touch, smelling of silver and cigar smoke. 

This weapon has a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with it. Additionally, it deals an additional 1d6 fire damage on a hit. When you attune to the sword, you gain the following additional effects:

**Heat:**
The Ashbringer has a number of Heat points equal to your Charisma modifier. Whenever you enter combat, these points recharge. Whenever you hit a creature with this weapon, you can expend 1 point of Heat choose to inflict Burn (3) instead of dealing the additional fire damage.

**Valor:**
Whenever you would miss an attack with this weapon, you can use your reaction to expend 2 points of Heat to add 1d6 to the attack roll, potentially causing the attack to hit. When you do, the attack loses the additional fire damage.

**Into Ashes:**
Whenever you reduce a creature to 0 hit points with this weapon, you can choose to incinerate the creature, turning it and any non magical equipment it is wearing into ashes. When you do, you regain all points of Heat.
