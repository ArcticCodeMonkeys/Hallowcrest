
*Rare, Boots (2000gp), (Requires Attunement)*

These heavy working boots have heavy duty thrusters strapped to the side of them, held on by a loose bracket. The boots have 7 charges which recharge on a long rest.

**Clank:** While wearing these boots you have disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.

**Thrusters:** While wearing these boots, you can cast the following spells targeting yourself without providing any components by expending the indicated number of charges:

-          Jump (2 Charges)
-          Levitate (3 Charges)
-          Fly (5 Charges)

**Burst of Flight:** While wearing these boots, you can expend 1 charge as a bonus action to take the Dash Action, boosting your movement as the thrusters propel you forward.
