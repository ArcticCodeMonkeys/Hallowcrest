Common, Martial Melee Weapon (10 gp).

| Damage          | Weight | Properties |
| --------------- | ------ | ---------- |
| 1d4 bludgeoning | 2 lb.  | Special    |

**Special.** If Hidden when making an attack with this weapon, its damage is instead 2d4 slashing and is Grappled on a hit. While Grappled this way, attacks with this weapon against the target automatically hit and deal 2d4 slashing damage.
