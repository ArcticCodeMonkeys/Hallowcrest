*Common, Light Crossbow (50gp)*

A peculiar device built with gear mechanisms and a short spool of rope. Whenever you make a ranged weapon attack with this weapon within the short range, you can choose to attach the spooled rope to the projectile, Hooking the target on a hit.

While Hooked, the target cannot move away from you. The condition ends if the target is forced out of the short range of your crossbow. Alternatively, the creature can make a DC 13 Strength *(Athletics)* check as an Action, taking 1d6 piercing damage and ending the condition.

As a bonus action, you can reel the Hooked target in, forcing it to make a DC 13 Strength Saving Throw or be pulled up to 10 feet towards you. On a failure, the target takes 1d4 piercing damage and is no longer Hooked.

After you make the Hook Shot, you must spend 1 minute respooling some rope in order to make the attack again.
