*Common, Light Crossbow (50gp)*

A peculiar device built with gear mechanisms and a short spool of rope. Whenever you make a ranged weapon attack with this weapon within the short range, you can choose to attach the spooled rope to the projectile and cause the target to be grappled by the bolt on a hit and remains grappled while it remains within the short range. The creature can make a DC 13 Strength *(Athletics)* check at the end of its turn to remove the bolt, taking 1d6 piercing damage and ending the grapple.

As a bonus action, you can reel the grappled target it, forcing it to make a DC 13 Strength Saving Throw or be pulled up to 10 feet towards you. On a failure, the target takes 1d4 piercing damage and is no longer grappled.

After you make the grapple attack, you must spend 1 minute respooling some rope in order to make the attack again.
