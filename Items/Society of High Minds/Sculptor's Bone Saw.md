
Very Rare (Sickle) (Minor Attunement)

This sickle has a +2 bonus to damage and attack rolls. Additionally, whenever you hit with an attack using this weapon, you can choose one of the following effects.

**Dissect**: You learn one resistance or immunity the target has.
**Rend**: You remove one resistance or immunity from the target that you learned through Dissect.
**Graft**: You attach a harmful appendage to the target. It takes 1d8 bludgeoning damage at the start of each of its turns until it or another creature uses an Action to remove the limb.
**Sew**: You sew the lips or fingers or the target together, making unable to perform the Verbal or Somatic components of spells. A creature can choose to rip the stitches at the start of its turn, taking 1d10 slashing damage and ending the effect.
