
*Uncommon, Consumable (200gp)*

This spray has enough fluid for 3 sprays, stored in a small modified perfume bottle. You can take the Utilize action to spray the fluid at a target within 10 feet. The creature must make a DC 13 Constitution Saving Throw or be blinded and fall prone. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the blinded condition on a success.
