Common, Wonderous Item (80 gp)

**Utilize.** You light the fuse of the bomb. At the end of your turn it detonates. If a creature is holding it, they take 1d6 bludgeoning damage. The area within a 20 foot emanation is filled with smoke as described by the Fog Cloud spell. It lasts for 1d4 + 1 rounds or until a strong wind dispels it.

**Throw.** As a part of an attack made using the Attack action, you can throw the bomb to a point you can see within 30 feet, causing it to instantly detonate as described above.
