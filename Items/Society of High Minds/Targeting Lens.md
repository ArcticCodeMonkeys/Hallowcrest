
Goggles (Uncommon) (Requires Minor Attunement)

These lenses use a combination of divination and illusion magic to analyze a creature's weaknesses and strengths and display them upon the lens. As a bonus action, you can target a creature you can see within 120 feet with the lens and learn the one of the following pieces of information about it:

- The creature's current and maximum hit points
- Any resistances, immunities or vulnerabilities the creature has
- Its highest and lowest ability score

For the next minute, or until you can no longer see the creature, you can use a bonus action to learn the same piece of information, or another from the list. Once you use the lens, it cannot be used again until you finish a long rest.

Each time you use the lens, roll a d20. On a 1, it shatters and explodes, dealing 1d4 piercing and 1d4 lightning damage to you and all creatures within 5ft, destroying the lens.
