
*Uncommon, Bracer / Necklace (300gp)*

![[Armor Up Amulet.jpg]]
Using an extradimensional space and transmutation magic, this amulet is capable of storing an entire suit of armor and a shield. The amulet comes in a Bracer and Necklace form and can be activated by stating the command word (designated by its creator) and grabbing the amulet as a bonus action. Doing so deploys the armor and shield held within upon the wearer, instantly equipping it for them. You can repeat this process to remove the armor and shield and store it within the amulet again.
