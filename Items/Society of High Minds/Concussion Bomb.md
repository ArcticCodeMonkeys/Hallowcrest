Uncommon, Wonderous Item (350 gp)

**Utilize.** You light the fuse of the bomb. At the end of your turn it detonates. If a creature is holding it, they take 1d6 bludgeoning damage.

**Throw.** As a part of an attack made using the Attack action, you can throw the bomb to a point you can see within 30 feet, causing it to instantly detonate.

When the bomb detonates, all creatures within a 15 foot radius of it must make a DC 15 Constitution Saving Throw or be Dazed for 1d4 rounds.
