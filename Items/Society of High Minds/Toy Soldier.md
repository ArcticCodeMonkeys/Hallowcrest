*Uncommon, Wonderous Item (100 gp)*

**Utilize.** You wind up this small toy soldier and issue it a basic command, such as "Fetch that apple", or "Find a key". The soldier then follows the order to the best of its ability, able to move up to 120 feet before it powers down. The Soldier has 14 AC and 1 hit point, and a 4 (-3) in all ability scores.
