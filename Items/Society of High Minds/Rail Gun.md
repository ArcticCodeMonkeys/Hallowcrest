*Uncommon, Wonderous Item (400gp) (Requires Attunement)*

This weapon has a range of (90/300), the Heavy, Two Handed, and Loading Properties, and deals 1d8 lightning damage on a hit. It also has 6 charges, which recharge on a long rest.

**Overcharge:** You can expend up to 3 charges to overcharge the weapon and fire a powerful shot which deals and additional 2d6 lightning damage for each charge expended. You must do so before the attack is rolled, and if you roll equal to or lower than the number of charges expended, the gun malfunctions, requiring you to spend a bonus action to make a DC 10 Tinkerer’s Tools check to be able to overcharge it again.

**Self-Destruct:** As a reaction to dropping to 0 hit points, you can let go of the safety on the weapon, triggering the self-destruct sequence, all creatures within 15 feet of you must make a dexterity saving throw or take 6d6 lightning damage + 2d6 lightning damage for each charge remaining, or half as much on a success, after which the item is destroyed.
