*Uncommon, Wonderous Item (100gp)*

You can spend 1 minute attaching this item to yourself. You can only have one Blood Transfuser attached at a time. Whenever you drop to 0 hit points, you can first make a Constitution Saving Throw with a DC equal to half the damage taken, or 10, whichever is higher. On a success, you instead fall to 1 hit point. Once you do so, the item becomes non-magical.
