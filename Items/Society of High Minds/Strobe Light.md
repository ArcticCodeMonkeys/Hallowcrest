*Uncommon, Consumable (150gp)*

**Utilize.** You power up the light and cause it to strobe violently in a 30 foot cone. All creatures in the area must make a DC 15 Constitution Saving Throw. On a failure, a target's movement speed is halved and it has a d4 penalty to attack rolls. On a failure by 5 or more, the target is Blinded until the start of your next turn.
