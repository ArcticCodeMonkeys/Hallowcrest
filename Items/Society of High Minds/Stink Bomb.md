*Uncommon, Wonderous Item (250 gp)*

**Utilize.** You light the fuse of the bomb. At the end of your turn it detonates. If a creature is holding it, they take 1d6 bludgeoning damage. 

**Throw.** In place of an attack made as part of the Attack action, you can throw the Stink Bomb to a point you can see within 30 feet and cause it to detonate. 

When the bomb detonates, the area in a 15 foot emanation from it is filled with nauseating gas as described in the Stinking Cloud spell. The gas lasts for 1 minute, or until a strong wind (such as the one created by Gust of Wind) disperses it.
