
(Rarity Varies), Martial Wraps, (Requires Attunement)

![[Spellbound Wraps.jpg]]

Your unarmed strikes made while wearing these wraps are considered magical for the purposes of overcoming resistances. You gain a bonus to hit and damage rolls with your unarmed strikes. Additionally, once per long rest, whenever you make an unarmed strike, you can activate the runes on the wraps, casting the spell within instead of the normal effects of your unarmed strike. On a hit, the target suffers the effect of the spell.

Common: +0, Cantrip, DC 11, +3 spell attack

Uncommon: +1, 1st Level, DC 13, +5 spell attack

Rare: +2, 2nd level, DC 15, +7 spell attack

Very Rare: +3, 3rd level, DC 17, +9 spell attack

The spell must be on the wizard’s spell list and be from the evocation, necromancy, or illusion schools of magic and be capable of targeting the creature. If it would normally target multiple creatures or an area, it only targets the creature.
