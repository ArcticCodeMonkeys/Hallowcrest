
*Rare, Robe (1500gp) (Requires Attunement by a Spellcaster)*

![[Scrollkeeper Garment.jpg]]

While wearing this robe and not wearing any armor, you are under the effects of Mage Armor and have a +1 bonus to AC. 

Additionally, you can choose a cantrip, 1st level spell, and 2nd level spell to inscribe upon the garment. You must be able to cast the spell to inscribe it, or have someone else who can inscribe it for you. Alternatively, you can copy the spell from a spell scroll. 

You can cast the cantrip at will. You can also cast the chosen leveled spells once per long rest each without spending a spell slot or providing components. If you are a wizard, these spells are also prepared and you can cast them using your Spellcasting feature.
