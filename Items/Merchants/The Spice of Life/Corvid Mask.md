
Uncommon, Mask (400gp) (Requires Half Attunement)

This mask resembling the visage of a crow is filled with herbs and spices to keep toxic fumes out while looking quite intimidating. While attuned to this mask, you have advantage on saving throws against being poisoned and immunity to poison damage caused by a gas. Additionally, you can add your constitution modifier to Intimidation checks you make while wearing the mask.
