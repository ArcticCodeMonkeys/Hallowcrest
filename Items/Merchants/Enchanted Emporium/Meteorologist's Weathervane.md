
*Uncommon, Quarterstaff (500gp), (Requires Attunement by a Spell Caster)*

This crooked weathervane has been repurposed into a quarterstaff and spell casting focus.

**Weather Forecast:** You learn the following spells and can cast them once per day each: Create or Destroy Water, Witch Bolt, Fog Cloud.

**Conducting Lightning:** Whenever you cast a spell which deals lightning damage while holding this staff, it gains a number of Lightning Charges equal to the spell’s level (1 for cantrips). Once per long rest, as a reaction to taking lightning damage, you can cast Absorb Elements from the staff without expending a spell slot, and gain 1d4 Lightning Charges. Lightning Charges decrease by 1 at the start of your turn and reset to 0 whenever you roll initiative.
