
Uncommon, Potion (250gp)

Whenever you drink this potion, you must make a DC 16 Constitution Saving Throw or begin hiccupping uncontrollably. On a failed save, you trigger a greater wild magic surge at the start of each of your turns. At the end of your turn, you must repeat the save, ending the effect on a success. On a critical failure, all creatures within 15 feet of you trigger a greater wild magic surge at the start of their turns. The potion lasts up to 1 minute or until dispelled.
