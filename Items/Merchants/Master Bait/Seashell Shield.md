
*Uncommon, Shield (150gp)*

"_She sells Seashell Shields on the she shor… damn it_"

This shield is composed of the thick and pearlescent material of seashells. As a reaction to being hit by an attack, you can shoot a wave of water out of the shield and back at the attacker. The attacker must make a DC 13 Strength Saving Throw or be knocked prone.
