
Uncommon, Consumable (200gp)
![[Sugarettes.jpg]]
These sticks of sugar are stored in a box and decorated to look like cigarettes. As an action, you can place one in your mouth and inhale, filling your lungs with sweet smoke and rendering it non-magical. As a bonus action for the next minute, you can cast Fog Cloud centered on yourself, with a 10 foot radius instead of its usual 20 feet.
