Rare, Wonderous Item (1400 gp) (Requires Attunement)

**Quick Draw.** Whenever you roll initiative, you can make one ranged weapon attack against a creature within range.

**Plenty o' Shells.** You ignore the loading property of ranged weapons and have an unlimited source of basic ammunition when firing ranged weapons.

**Worn n' Torn.** You can add your Charisma modifier to Survival checks and have advantage on saving throws made to resist hot climates.
