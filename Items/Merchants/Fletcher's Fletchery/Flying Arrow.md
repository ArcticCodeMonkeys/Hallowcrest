*Uncommon, Wondrous Item (250 gp) (Requires Minor Attunement)*

While attuned to this item, you can begin a low frequency whistle which causes the arrow to begin to float. To do so, you must provide verbal components and maintain concentration as if you were concentrating on a spell. As a bonus action while whistling, you can increase the pitch and cause the arrow to dart in a 30 foot line. All creatures in the area take 1d6 piercing damage.

The arrow can remain in flight for up to 1 minute per long rest, after which its magic must recharge.
