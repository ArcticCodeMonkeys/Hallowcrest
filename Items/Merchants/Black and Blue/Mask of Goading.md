
*Uncommon, Mask (450gp)*

"_Has anyone ever told you you have a very punchable face?_"

While wearing this mask, others perceive you as the person they hate the most, though they realize you are simply wearing a mask. You have disadvantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks. As a bonus action, you can target one creature within 30 feet of you which can see you and force it to make a DC 13 Wisdom Saving Throw. On a failure, the creature has disadvantage on all attack rolls against creatures other than you until the end of its next turn.
