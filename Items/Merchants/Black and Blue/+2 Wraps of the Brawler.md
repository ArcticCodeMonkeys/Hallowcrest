Rare (Wraps), (Requires Attunement)

While wearing these wraps, you have a +2 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls with unarmed strikes. Whenever you hit a creature with an unarmed strike, you can force them to make a DC 16 Constitution Saving Throw or be Dazed until the end of their next turn. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per half rest.
