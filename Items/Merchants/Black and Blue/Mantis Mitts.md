Rare, Gloves (2000gp), (Requires Attunement)

These boxing gloves are made from the shells of the might mantis shrimp, able to allow the wearer to punch at incredible speeds. 

While attuned to these gloves, you do not suffer any penalties to the attack rolls of unarmed strikes made underwater, and when you do, your reach with unarmed strikes increases by 10 feet. Once per turn, when you hit a creature with these gloves, it must succeed a DC 15 Constitution Saving Throw or be pushed back up to 15 feet and be knocked prone.
