
Rare, Wonderous Item (4000gp)

These bandages have magical healing properties, able to speed up the natural recovery process. While wearing them over the course of a long rest, you can end one of the following effects ailing you:

- 1 Level of Exhaustion
- 1 Level of Injured
- 1 condition
- Any reduction to ability score or maximum hit points

Once the bandages have been used, they cannot be used for the next 24 hours.
