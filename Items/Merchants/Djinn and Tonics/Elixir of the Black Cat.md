*Uncommon, Potion (250gp)*

Whenever you drink this potion, you and all creatures within 15 feet of you treat any roll of 15 or higher on a d20 test as a 15. Whenever you roll a critical failure on a d20 test, all creatures within 15 feet of you gain disadvantage on their next d20 test. This potion lasts up to 1 minute or until dispelled.
