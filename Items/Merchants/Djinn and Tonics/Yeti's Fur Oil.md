Common, Oil (20gp)

![[Yeti's Fur Oil.jpg]]

This grey slimy and thick oil reeks of sweat and wet fur. It is very cold to the touch and must be lathered on in clumps. 

Over the course of a short or long rest, this oil can be applied to a Large or smaller creature, granting it resistance to cold damage and vulnerability to fire damage for the next 24 hours, or until it spends a short or long rest removing the oil.
