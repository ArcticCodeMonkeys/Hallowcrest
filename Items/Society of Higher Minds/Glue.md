*Mundane, Consumable (35 gp)*

A tube of glue has 5 charges.

**Utilize.** You lather the glue across a smooth and clean surface, such as a door, wall, or floor, covering up to 5 square feet. When a creature touches that surface, it must succeed a DC (10 + 1 per charge expended) Strength Save or be stuck to it. While stuck, the creature cannot move and has a d4 penalty to Dexterity Saving Throws. It can expend all of its movement to repeat the saving throw. On a success, it moves 5 feet in a direction of its choosing and is no longer stuck.
