*Rare, Cloak, (2000gp), (Requires Attunement)*

While wearing this coat, you gain the following benefits.

**Flame Resistant**. You gain resistance to fire damage.

**Feel the Flame**. Whenever you deal fire damage, you gain a level of Burning.

**Heat Transfer**. Whenever you deal fire damage, you can remove all levels of Burning on yourself and inflict them upon one target.
