*Rare, Amulet, (4000gp), (Requires Attunement)*

While wearing this amulet, you produce an aura of warmth in a 15 foot emanation. Creatures of your choosing in the aura gain the following benefits.

**Warmth.** You are immune to the harmful effects of cold weather.

**Toasty.** You are immune to the Chilled and Burning conditions.

**Revitalized.** Whenever a creature in the emanation deals fire damage, you regain 1d4 + 2 hit points.
