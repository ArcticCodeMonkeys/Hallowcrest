Uncommon, Tattoo (400gp), (Requires Attunement)

These tattoos come in varying colors, representing one of the 5 chromatic dragons. The color of the tattoo determines its damage type (Fire, Cold, Acid, Poison, or Lightning).

While attuned to this tattoo, you gain the following effects:

**Draconic Resilience:** Whenever you take damage of your tattoo’s type, you can use your reaction to reduce the damage by 1d8 + your constitution modifier.

**Breath Weapon:** Once per half rest, you can enter an ascended dragon state as a bonus action. For the next minute, you are under the effects of the Dragon’s Breath spell, with the damage type of your tattoo’s type.
