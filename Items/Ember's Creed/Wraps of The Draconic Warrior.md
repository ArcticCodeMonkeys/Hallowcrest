
Rare Wraps (Requires Attunement)

These wraps grant a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with your unarmed strikes.

While wearing these wraps, you can add your Wisdom or Strength Modifier to Charisma (Intimidation) checks. Additionally, you gain the following features:

**Ascending Dragon**: You leap into the air and cast a shadow over your enemies. By expending 30 feet of movement, you move straight up 30 feet and begin to hover until the end of your current turn. All creatures within 5 feet of where you left must succeed a DC 15 Strength Saving Throw or be knocked prone.

**Descending Flame**: You imitate the swooping descent of a dragon releasing its firey breath upon its prey. While Ascending Dragon is active, land at a point on the ground within 15 feet without taking falling damage. Additionally, in place of an unarmed strike, you can force all creatures within 10 feet of the point where you land to succeed a DC 15 Dexterity Saving Throw or take 2d6 fire damage.

**Flyby**: Whenever you hit a creature with an attack, it cannot target you with opportunity attacks this turn.
