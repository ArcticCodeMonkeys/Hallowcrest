*Rare, Wonderous Item, (4000gp), (Requires Minor Attunement)*

This miraculous invention spews flaming oil to ignite targets and light aflame large areas. 

The Flamespitter has 7 charges which recharge by replenishing its oil over the course of a short or long rest (5gp per charge). As an action, you can fire the Flamespitter in either a line or a cone by expending one charge. The Flamespitter ignites flamable objects and deals double damage to them.

**Line.** All creatures in a 5 foot wide, 20 foot long line must succeed a DC 16 Dexterity Saving Throw or take 3d6 fire damage and gain Burning (3). On a success, the target takes half as much damage and gains Burning (1).

**Cone.** All creatures in a 15 foot cone must succeed a DC 16 Constitution Saving Throw or take 4d8 fire damage and gain Burning (1). On a success, the target only takes half as much damage.

**Self-Destruct.** As an action, you can ignite the entire Flamespitter and throw it to a point within 20 feet. All creatures in a 15 foot radius must succeed a DC 16 Dexterity Saving Throw or take 2d8 fire damage and gain a level of Burning for each charge remaining in the Flamespitter. On a success, the target takes half as much damage. The Flamespitter is then destroyed.
