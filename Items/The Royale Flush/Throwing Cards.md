*Common, Melee Weapon (45gp).*

This deck of 52 cards is a set of lethal weapons. You are considered proficient with these cards if have proficiency with darts, daggers, or gambling sets. Once a card is thrown, it becomes non-magical.

_Melee Weapon (simple)_

**Damage:** 1d4

**Damage Type:** Slashing

**Properties:** Finesse, Light, Range, Thrown

**Range:** 20/60

If you are proficient with gambling sets, you can use the following weapon mastery.

**Straight Flush:**

Whenever you hit a creature with a thrown weapon attack with these cards on your turn, until the end of your turn the cards deal an additional d4 damage. If you roll maximum damage on both dice, it deals an additional d4 damage.
