Uncommon, Consumable (100gp)

As a reaction to making a d20 test, you can flip this coin. On heads, the roll is treated as a 20. On tails, the roll is treated as a 1. After the coin is flipped, it becomes a non magical silver piece. Attempting to use more than one of these coins in a period of less than 24 hours results in the user always getting tails.
