Rare Dagger, (Requires Attunement)

This dagger has a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls. The short and long range for the thrown property of this dagger are doubled (40/120). On a hit or miss with a thrown attack using this dagger, it returns to your hand.

**Empaler.** On a hit, you can choose to *impale* the target, leaving the dagger stuck in their flesh. The target takes 1d4 piercing damage at the start of each of its turns while *impaled*. A creature within 5 feet of the target can pull the dagger out in place of an attack, dealing 3d4 piercing damage and ending the *impaled* condition. You can use this feature twice per short rest.
