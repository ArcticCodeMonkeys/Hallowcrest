
Consumable (Uncommon)

As an action you can present the cards and throw them into the air making a DC 15 Sleight of Hand, Performance, or Arcana check. On a success, you become invisible for the next minute, or until you make an attack or cast a spell, and teleport to an unoccupied space you can see within 30 feet. The space where you left is heavily obscured until the start of your next turn. On a failure, the space is still heavily obscured, but you do not become invisible or teleport.
