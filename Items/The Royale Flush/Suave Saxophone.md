*Rare, Musical Instrument, (3500gp), (Requires Attunement)*

This musical instrument gives its user an aura of suaveness and effortlessness that is unmatched, all the while making a tune that is near irresistible. The saxophone has 7 charges, which recharge upon completing a long rest, and can be used for the following features. Some features may require a saving throw, the DC for which is determined by the result of a Charisma (Performance) check, up to a maximum of 20.

**Let's Groove (1).**  As a Magic Action, you can end all effects reducing or setting a creatures speed within 30 feet. All creatures of your choosing must succeed a Wisdom saving throw or immediately move 10 feet towards you (if there is space).

**Enticing Performance (3).** As a Magic Action, creatures of your choice that can hear you must succeed a Wisdom saving throw or be charmed by you. While charmed, creatures cannot willingly move away from you. Charmed creatures can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of their turns, ending the effect on a success. The performance lasts for 1 minute.

**Smooth Jazz (3).**  As a Magic Action, you cast Calm Emotions without expending a spell slot, targeting any creatures of your choice that can hear you.

**Can't Stop the Music (7).** As a Magic Action, you cast Otto's Irresistible Dance without expending a spell slot. When the creature succeeds the saving throw to end the spell, the spell transfers to a different creature of your choosing within 10 feet, provided you are still concentrating on it, and does not end. 

**Sax Battle.** Once per long rest, at the start of your turn, you can enter a Sax Battle with another creature you can see also holding a Suave Saxophone and make a contested Charisma (Performance) check. On a success, the creature is Stunned until the end of its next turn. On a failure, you are Stunned until the end of your next turn instead.
