*Rare, Musical Instrument, (3500gp), (Requires Attunement)*

This musical instrument gives its user an aura of suaveness and effortlessness that is unmatched, all the while making a tune that is near irresistible. The saxaphone has 7 charges, which recharge upon completing a long rest, and can be used for the following features. Some features may require a saving throw, the DC for which is determined by a contested Charisma (Performance) check. The result of this check can never be higher than 20.

**Let's Groove (1).** As a Magic Action, you can end all effects reducing or setting a creatures speed within 30 feet. All creatures of your choosing must succeed a Wisdom saving throw or immediately move 10 feet towards you (if there is space).

**Enticing Performance (3).** As a Magic Action, creatures of your choice that can hear you must succeed a Wisdom saving throw or be charmed by you. While charmed, creatures have disadvantage on attack rolls and they cannot willingly move away from you. Charmed creatures can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of their turns, ending the effect on a success. The performance lasts for 1 minute.

**Smooth Jazz (3).**  As a Magic Action, you cast Calm Emotions without expending a spell slot, targeting creatures of your choice that can hear you.
