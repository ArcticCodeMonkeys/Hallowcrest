Rare Dagger, (Requires Minor Attunement)

While holding this +2 dagger, which resembles a very short rapier, in your off hand you are able to use it for defensive maneuvers. As a reaction to being targeted by a melee weapon attack, you can use your reaction to attempt to deflect it, increasing your AC by 2 for the triggering attack. If this bonus causes the attack to miss, you make make one melee weapon attack with the dagger against the attacker, provided it is within reach.
