*Uncommon, Daggers (400gp), (Requires Attunement)*

These daggers are fitted with special arm mounts, allowing them to be easily concealed up a sleeve. A creature attempting to locate these daggers must succeed a DC 15 Study check in order to do so. Each dagger is has a +1 magical bonus to hit and damage rolls, and has an additional effect, listed below:

**Spade:**
The spade dagger is a sharp and dark black stone blade. Whenever you hit a creature with an attack from this blade, the target must succeed a DC 13 Constitution Saving Throw or begin Bleeding. A Bleeding creature takes 1d4 piercing damage at the start of each of its turns, and must repeat the saving throw, ending the effect on a success. The effect also ends if the creature regains hit points.

**Club:**
The club dagger is a three pronged Sai, whenever you hit a creature with a dagger of a different suit, you can make an attack with this dagger as a bonus action, and the attack is made at advantage.

**Heart:**
The heart dagger is a thin needle like blade. Whenever you hit a creature with this dagger, it must succeed a DC 13Wisdom Saving Throw or be charmed by you until the end of its next turn.

**Diamond:**
The diamond dagger is a heavy parrying dagger with a blunter blade than the rest. Whenever you take bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage from a melee weapon attack, you can use a reaction to reduce the damage by 1d4.
