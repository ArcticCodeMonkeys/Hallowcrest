*Rare, Wonderous Item, (3500gp), (Requires Attunement)*

This crooked finger comes from a hag and must replace an existing finger as a part of the attunement process. While attuned, the Crooked Finger has 9 charges, which can be used for the following benefits.

**Hag's "Gifts".** As a bonus action, you can bestow a gift to one ally within 30 feet by expending 1 charge. The target gains advantage on saving throws of an ability score of your choosing and disadvantage on saving throws of two ability scores of your choosing. This effect lasts for 1 minute or until you end it at the start of your turn.

**Curses Upon Thee.** You can cast Bestow Curse without expending a spell slot by expending 3 charges.

**Croak**. You can cast Polymorph without expending a spell slot by expending 5 charges. When you do, you must choose a Frog as the targets new form.

**A Deadly Bargain.** You can cast Finger of Death without expending a spell slot by expending 9 charges. When you do, the spell also targets you.
