
Uncommon, Consumable (150gp)


![[Lockmelter.jpg]]


A key with a small vial of highly corrosive acid in the handle. As a bonus action, it can be inserted into any normal sized lock, causing the acid to pour through the length of the key and out of the small holes along the inside. The acid begins to melt the lock from the inside, roll 4d4. The DC to force the door open decreases by that much and it becomes impossible to lockpick. This process destroys the key. This key does not work on magical locks or locks under the effect of magical wards such as Arcane Lock.
