*Rare, Amulet, (1500gp), (Requires Minor Attunement)*

Whenever you deal damage with an unarmed strike or natural weapon attack, you can gain one of the following benefits.

**Satiated.** You regain hit points equal to 1d6 + your constitution modifier.

**Bloodthirsty.** You gain advantage on your next attack roll with an unarmed strike or natural weapon within the next minute.

**Voracious.** You deal additional damage equal to twice your constitution modifier.

You can use each of these features once per short rest.
