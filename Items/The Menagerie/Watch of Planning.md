Wonderous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement)

This clockwork stopwatch is functions as a normal watch with a very high degree of precision. It can be used in the following ways:

**Sync Up**: By spending 1 minute with a willing creature, you can tie the watch to the creatures movements and actions. By designating one simple action, such as raising their hand, clenching their fist, or jumping, your watch will indicate when that creature takes that action.

**Prepare**: You take take the Ready action at any time. When you do, the Action does not expire at the start of your next turn, but instead whenever you take the Ready Action again.

**Minor Contingency**: You or a creature that holds the watch can cast a spell or 3rd level or lower into the watch and designate a quantifiable event to set as a trigger and a target. If the target is a creature, you must specify its name or type and if it is a point, you must specify a relative direction and distance. When the trigger occurs, the spell takes effect.
