There are a few main religions in Hallowcrest:

#### Worshipers of Gond:

Most artisans worship Gond and pray to him during or before their work. 

#### [[Ember's Creed]]:

Ember's Creed while a faction, is also the name of their belief. These worshipers revere dragons and see them as beautiful powerful creatures. They worship [[Searclaw]] for his acts of benevolence and bravery and often worship Bahamut as well. Some more fanatic members have a more destructive view point, believing that flame cleanses all, and that only through the ashes can evil be extinguished and come out pure and good.

#### Worshipers of Lolth:

Most members of [[Ne're Darke]] at least fear or respect Lolth. As Mother Matron of all drow, almost all drow or their affiliates worship her strength. [[Ne're Darke]] as a whole does not go deep into religious fanaticism though, and will often just pray to her for strength before a battle.

#### Worshipers of Torm:

Torm is a God worshiped by many people throughout the city, mostly members of the [[Sovereign Guard]]. Being a deity of duty and righteousness, he is an easy target for worship among the hopeful and defenders of the city. [[Gestalt Hallowbrand]] was famously a worshiper of Torm, and as such his legacy has spread the worship throughout the city.


Other religions exist, but are not extremely common. Most churches have smaller sections with effigies to other less common gods, while still being dedicated to a specific religion.
