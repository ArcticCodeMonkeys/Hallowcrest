Some of the most powerful warriors in history were only able to do so from the help of their Soul Bond. A Soul Bond is a type of creature who bonds tightly with the soul of another. Soul Bonds can only occur between powerful creatures with extremely strong shared convictions. They are also exceedingly rare. It is said that when one Soul Bond dies, the other loses a part of theirs, becoming a hollow shell of the person they once were. Below is every documented Soul Bond:
