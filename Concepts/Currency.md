[[Hallowcrest]] has some nicknames for its currency, which is issued by [[The Capital]].

- Copper Piece - Bit ("Two bits for a pint of ale? That's outrageous!"), named for their small worth
- Silver Piece - Chip ("5 Chips says he slips and dies"), named for their often poor quality
- Gold Piece - Crown ("My new gig pays 5 crowns a week, not bad right?"), named for the crown symbol imprinted upon the coin
- Platinum Piece - Dragon ("Did you hear that new house is going for 12 Dragons?"), named for their extreme rarity.
