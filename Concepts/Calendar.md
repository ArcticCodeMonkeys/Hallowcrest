
The Year is split up into 10 months, each are 30 days, plus Genesis, which is a half week (5 day) long period between years dedicated to new beginnings, for a total of 305 days a year. Every 14 years, Genesis gains an extra day. Weeks are measured in Tendays, aptly 10 days long in which 7 out of the 10 are dedicated to work.
