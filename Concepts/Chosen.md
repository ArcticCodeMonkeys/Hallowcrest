
Certain people are paragons or embodiments of a certain aspect of existence. Perhaps someone is extremely lucky, or incredibly brave, or unspeakably evil. By displaying these acts of embodiment, they drew the attention of a god, who selects them to be their chosen. Gods which select mortals as their chosen usually do it to spread their word and domain, but sometimes do it as a part of a pact. A Chosen is usually a Warlock or Cleric, and less often a Sorcerer or Druid, though anyone can be a Chosen. In some rare cases, extremely powerful beings such as primordials or dragons can act as a Patron and select a Chosen, but they are often much weaker, as the power level of a Chosen is proportional to the strength of the Patron.
