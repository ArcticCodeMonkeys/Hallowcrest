A popular game among students of Aurora College, the game can be summarized as Capture the Flag but with magic.

Leagues:
There are three major leagues of Mage Tower.

College: Restricted to members of Aurora College, 4 Teams, one for each House
National: Restricted to citizens of Hallowcrest, the Capital, or Spine of the North, 6 teams

### The Goal:
The main goal of the game is to steal the Mascot of the other team(s), which is kept upon a large tower known as the team's Sanctum. Games usually go first to lead by three, or until the timer (40 minutes) runs out.

### The Arena:
The arena is composed of two or more Sanctums (one for each team), 120ft apart from each other and 60 feet tall. The arena is placed under a powerful ward, in which creatures gain 20 temporary hit points at the start of their turns, the terrain cannot be damaged (but can be altered), and the mascots cannot be destroyed. Additionally, the mascots instantly teleport back to their Sanctums once entering a different Sanctum, and magical effects are slowly dispelled by Arcane Decay. A creature which loses its temporary hit points is incapacitated until the end of its next turn. A creature that is incapacitated is unable to defend the Mascot, otherwise a contested Strength (Athletics) check can be made to attempt to pry it from someone's hands.

### Arcane Decay:
Spells and magical effects are subject to Arcane Decay, which helps keep the game moving and dynamic. Spells that have a duration longer than instantaneous and do not have concentration slowly decay and eventually fade. At the start of the caster's turn, it must make a spell casting ability check for each active spell fitting the description above it cast that is currently active. The DC for the check begins at 10, and increases by 3 for each turn the spell has been active. Additionally, effects which incapacitate or restrain end within 3 failed saves.

### Fouls:

Creatures incur fouls for:
- Breaking the rules
- Leaving the bounds of the arena
- Intentionally Harming or Killing an Opponent
- Damaging the Arena or harming the crowd
- Attempting to utilize magic to influence a referee
- Delay of Game: If no effect reasonably stops you from scoring a point and you do not attempt to do so.

A foul results in a player being targeted by a Banishment spell. They automatically fail the initial saving throw, and the DC for the repeat saves is 15. After 3 failed saves, the spell ends. If the player is holding a mascot, it is returned to its Sanctum, unless it is their own, in which case it drops to the ground.

### Game Structure:

Each match is split into two halves, and after 20 minutes, play continues until a team scores a point, after which the half is finished (this is called Overtime). During Overtime, a randomly selected effect occurs, slightly changing the rules of the game.
If a team ever has a 3 point lead, or if both halves finish, the game ends. If the game would end in a tie, Overtime continues. At the start each half, teams must return to their Sanctum and have 10 minutes to prepare. Each team is allotted 1 time out, lasting 1 minute, which can only be taken directly after an enemy team scores a point against the team which wishes to use the timeout. If a team has the mascot when a time out is called, it is returned back to its Sanctum, along with all of the players. 


### Overtime:
During overtime, play continues until one team scores a point. At the beginning of Overtime, randomly select an effect from this list (24), changing the rules of the game.

- Short and Sweet: All Sanctums becomes shrink to ground level
- Mayhem: Fouls will not be called except for influencing officials
- Wild Magic: All spells trigger wild magic surges
- Paranoid: Only one player from each time may leave the Sanctum at a time. Players other than the designated player outside of the Sanctum at the start of overtime must spend their turn returning to their Sanctum by the fastest means possible, until they reach the Sanctum.
- Risky: No players may remain within 10 feet of their own Sanctum, unless they possess an enemy Mascot.
- Fleet of Foot: All players have their movement speeds increased by 10 feet and can Dash as a bonus action
- Big Red: A young red dragon enters the arena. It targets players with mascots with range, and otherwise targets randomly.
- Angry Mascots: When a mascot is brought to an enemy sanctum, it transforms into a mimic which must be slain in order to score the point.
- Fanfare: Deafening music blares, deafening all creatures and making Counterspell uncastable.
- Keep Away: All mascots instantly teleport to the center of the arena. At the end of every round roll a d20. On a 5 or lower, it all mascots return to the center again.
- Commoners: Leveled spells cannot be cast (other than your signature spell).
- Fast Paced: All creatures gain an additional bonus action and reaction each turn.
- Slimy: The ground is covered in slime, making the entire arena difficult terrain.
- Wrong Turn: All sanctums rotate clockwise in position at the end of each round. Creatures and mascots within the sanctum move with it.
- Thunderdomes: Sanctums are surrounded by a wall of magical force as described by the Wall of Force spell. These walls have 14 AC and 50 hit points.
- Mischievous Mike: A quickling named Mike is released into the arena, it attempts to steal any mascots not within a sanctum.
- Arcane Sustanance: Arcane Decay is disabled
- Play of the Game: The next point scored is worth 2
- Point Stealer: The next team to score a point can remove a point from a team of their choice.
- Tower Defense: Turrets appear at the top of each tower. On initative 20 they target the closest enemy player and make a ranged spell attack (+7). On a hit, the target is pushed back 30 feet and knocked prone.
- Inflatable Mascots: The Mascots are permenantly under the effects of the Levitate spell. Players carrying the Mascots must succeed a DC 15 Strength Saving Throw at the start of each of their turns or be affected by it while they hold the mascot.
- Who Turned Off the Lights: The entire arena is cloaked in darkness.
- Unstoppable: Temporary hit points are increased by 10 and the incapacitated condition ends at the end of the targets next turn.
- Life Link: Whenever one player takes damage, all of its team members take the same amount of damage.
- Powerful Spells: Each creature can pick a new signature spell, this time up to 5th level.
- Lost Pet: A ownerless mascot appears in the center of the arena, it can be scored for a point by any team.


### Setup:

Teams known as Studies are formed of 4 students, each serving a key position. Players choose their position before the game starts, and after each point scored (as well as at the start of the game), choose a Boon, a Team-Up, and a Signature Spell.


#### Positions:

##### Warden:
The Defender of the Mascot, while able to leave the sanctum, the Warden usually doesn't go far. The Warden is allowed to prepare only abjuration and conjuration spells, and has a +2 bonus to AC and saving throws.

- **Stalwart**: You are immune to effects that would forcably move you.
- **Resilient:** Once per game, when you fail a d20 test, you succeed instead (except against fouls).
- **Fortified:** You have advantage on grapple checks and Strength Saving Throws.


##### Evoker:
The Diversion and Distracter, usually not directly attempting for the mascot but looking to throw off the other team, and usually remains around center field causing issues for travelling opponents. The Evoker is allow to prepare only evocation and illusion spells, and as a +2 Bonus to Spell Attack Rolls and Spell Save DC.

- **Influx:** Once per point, you can automatically score a critical hit, you are then incapacitated until the end of your next turn.
- **Distracting:** Once per point you can force a creature who failed a saving throw against a spell you cast to look towards you. Its movement speed is halved and it has disadvantage on saving throws until the end of its next turn.
- **Confusing:** Illusion Spells you cast cannot be nullified or have its effect negated by physically interacting with them.

##### Weaver:
The Thief and Extractor, usually the one to get the mascot, at least at first. The Weaver is allowed to prepare only illusion and transmutation spells, and has a +10ft movement speed and advantage on Dexterity and Strength Saving Throws.

- **Adaptability**: Whenever you fail a saving throw against a spell, you have advantage on your next saving throw against that spell.
- **Ethereal:** You cannot be targeted by opportunity attacks and are resistant to physical damage types.
- **Subtle:** Once per point, you can cast a spell that normally has verbal components without providing them.

##### Diviner:
The Planner and Buffer, usually stays far away from the other players and looks to assist the entire team, while focusing on maintaining concentration. The Diviner is allowed to prepare only divination and enchantment spells.

- **Portent:** Once per point, roll a d20. Replace the result of any d20 test with this roll, after which you have disadvantage on d20 tests until the end of your next turn.
- **Focused:** Once per point, whenever you fail a concentration check, you can instead succeed and gain a +1d4 bonus to your next concentration check.
- **Warding:** Allies under the effects of one of your spells gain 25 temporary hit points at the start of each of their turns instead of 20.

#### Signature Spells:

Each player can choose a signature spell, this spell can be cast once per point without expending a spell slot and can be at most 2nd level. Every creature also gains Counterspell and Dispel Magic as a signature spell.

#### Team-Up

Each team chooses one of these team-up effects at the start of the game. Teams can create their own Team Up Effects by spending 20 hours practicing together.

- **Tight-Knit:** While within 10 feet of an ally, you and the creature are under the effects of Warding Bond.
- **Careful:** You have advantage on saving throws against spells your ally casts.
- **Supportive:** As an action once per point, each player can grant another player a d8 bonus to the next d20 test it makes.
- **Alleyoop:** Once per point, whenever a player holding a mascot becomes incapacitated, it can throw the mascot to another player within 15ft.
- **Switcheroo:** Once per game, if a player holding a mascot moves within reach of another player, it can secretly pass the mascot to the other player. Creatures must succeed a DC 18 Perception check to notice you the original player does not have the mascot.
- **Hold Down The Fort:** While two or more allies are within the Sanctum, they have a +2 bonus to Spell Save DC.
- **Fan Favorites:** The first time a player on your team grabs the mascot each round, your team gains a +d4 bonus to d20 tests until the start of the player's next turn as the crowd cheers.
- **Study Buddies:** Once per game, each player can cast a spell that a different player prepared, including their signature spell.
- **Under the Table:** Once per game, if a player would trigger a foul, any player can make a DC 16 Persuasion check. On a success, the foul is cancelled.
- **Home Team Advantage**: While within your sanctum, you have advantage on saving throws against spells.
