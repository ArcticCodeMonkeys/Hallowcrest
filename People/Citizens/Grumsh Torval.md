A Half Orc farmer and owner of [[Heritage Farms]]. Affiliated with [[The Keepers of Oak]]. His Daughter, [[Penelope Torval]] works as a stablehand at [[Horsin' Around]]. 

Really enjoys working on the farm, is periodically casted with Greater Restoration to ensure he never has to sleep or stop working. Incredibly muscular, friendly, and lonely.
