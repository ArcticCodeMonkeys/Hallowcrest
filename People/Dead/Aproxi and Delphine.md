![[Aproxi and Delphine.jpg]]Two lovers said to be the physical embodiments of love and affection. The tale of Aproxi and Delphine is well known, so much so that an idiom 'Aproxi and Delphine' (meaning two people who are lovestruck by one another) and the phrases 'My Aproxi' and 'Yours, Delphine' (meaning 'My Soulmate' and a loving way to sign a letter respectively) were sprouted from them. Aproxi and Delphine are also the only instance of two humanoids forming a [[Concepts/Soul Bond|Soul Bond]]. There is a yearly festival known as [[Adoration Day]] dedicated to them and love in general. People confess their love, hand out gifts of admiration, and the streets are filled with rose pedals which the children spread throughout the city.
