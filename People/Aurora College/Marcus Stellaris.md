![[Marcus Stellaris.jpeg]]Son of [[Aurora Stellaris]] and [[Gestalt Hallowbrand]], current dean of [[Factions/Aurora College]]. He is a strong intellectual and powerful mage, mostly focused on research and pushing the bounds of what is possible with magic. 

He does not care for his father, [[Gestalt Hallowbrand]]'s legacy, and wishes to make his own name for himself. As such, he has taken the last name of his mother instead. As such, he also does not have the best of relationships with his twin brother [[Elowyn Hallowbrand]], who feels his ignorance towards his father is disrespectful.

He invented the Magic Mouth, Levitate, and Animate Object spells and owns the [[Enchanted Emporium]]. He does not visit it much other than to collect profits, since it is run by animated objects with Magic Mouth programming. 

Before becoming dean at [[Locations/Hideouts/Aurora College|Aurora College]], Marcus worked closely with the [[Society of Higher Minds]] to diversify his studies. He became obsessed with a pattern he saw within magic and specifically magical artifacts from the [[Precursors]]. He was able to break down magic into three core principles: Power, Imbuement, and Connection. He believes that all magic can be described by these three aspects, and can be encoded through a set of runic symbols he calls [[Runes]]. After this discovery, the [[Society of Higher Minds]] boomed in popularity as it was able to begin creating magical blueprints and replicating some of the more simple [[Precursors]] artifacts. This also gave Marcus the respect of his mother and granted him the dean position. 

Marcus now looks to advance his research into separating these Runes from existing items and creating unique combinations, as well as inventing new runes and learning to decipher the more advanced runes of wonderous items and artifacts, such as [[The Ashen Horn]] or [[Items/Artifacts/The Sage Mind|The Sage Mind]]
