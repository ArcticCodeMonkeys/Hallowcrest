![[Aurora Stellaris.jpeg]]
Elf, Powerful Mage and Diviner and founder of [[Locations/Hideouts/Aurora College|Aurora College]]. She was the first to divine the plan of [[Ne're Darke]] to capture [[Daurgothoth]] and was a crucial actor in [[The Battle of Ash and Ember]], where she met [[Gestalt Hallowbrand]]. The two worked closely together and ultimately ended up conceiving twin sons just days before [[Gestalt Hallowbrand]]'s death.

Aurora was the first and only Lady of [[Hallowcrest]], holding the position from the establishment of [[Hallowcrest]] up until [[Elowyn Hallowbrand]] turned 17 and took the position in her stead. Due to a large influx of funding from [[The Capital]], Aurora did not have to make many difficult decisions as Lady, and focused on bringing research and innovation to [[Hallowcrest]], pitching it as a city of new beginnings. The stress of balancing being Lady and dean at [[Locations/Hideouts/Aurora College|Aurora College]] became too much for her, ultimately leading to her transferring the position to [[Elowyn Hallowbrand]].

She discovered [[Items/Artifacts/The Sage Mind|The Sage Mind]], in a ruins in the [[Golden Plains]] and has asked for it to be brought to [[Hallowcrest]] to be researched. 

Now retired, Aurora Stellaris has returned to her motherly role, trying to assist her twin sons  [[Elowyn Hallowbrand]] and [[Marcus Stellaris]] in their stressful roles. She also spends more time enjoying life, hosting and attending balls with many different noble families and diplomats. Makes often trips back and forth to [[The Capital]], but tries to avoid it now in her old age.

Invented multiple spells, including Blink, Gravity Sinkhole, Gravity Fissure, and Crown of Stars (her signature spell).
