Notorious Assassin for hire. Leaves a single black rose petal as a calling card and victims hear his whistle toned flute before they are killed. Commits most of his kills at dawn with a morning fog and targets rich nobles. Most commoners actually sort of treat him as a folk hero / legend, since he has never been arrested and keeps the upper class in check.

Confirmed Kills: 127
Wanted: Dead or Alive
Reward: 5000gp
