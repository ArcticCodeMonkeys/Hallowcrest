![[Lady Luck.jpg]]Half Elf, Cleric of Luck, Investor and frequent patron of [[Fortune's Favor]], Co-Leader of [[The Royale Flush]] along with [[The Maestro]].

Lady Luck is a [[Chosen]] of Tymora, who often is referred to by the same name. Lady Luck is extremely confident, believing that she was blessed with perfect luck since she was a young girl. She has never had any major setbacks in life and things always seemed to go her way. As such, she is also quite entitled and petty, and will act recklessly if anything ever does go wrong. She wields a deck of tarot cards which she uses to foretell the future of her allies and enemies and the Threads of Fate, which she pulls upon to bend her own luck. 

She met [[The Maestro]] in [[Hallowcrest]], as both of them came from [[The Capital]] to set up a casino due the lack of policy around gambling in [[Hallowcrest]]. The two heard word of each other starting their own casinos, [[Fortune's Favor]] and [[The Drifting Barrel]] (which eventually became a neighboring tavern), and decided to join efforts instead of competing. Together they grew [[Fortune's Favor]] into the largest casino in the world and began forming a group of mercenaries they call [[The Royale Flush]] to protect themselves against the criminal organizations of [[Hallowcrest]]. [[The Royale Flush]] don't take part in too much criminal activity themselves, besides the occasional high value heist from the [[Sealed Vault]] or some 'disappearances' of troublesome guests. They give large donations to the [[Sovereign Concord]] in order to dodge legislation and the patrol of [[Sovereign Guard]].

[[Lady Luck]] focuses on managing the casino floor, hosting events and generating revenue for the casino. She has no moral issues with the criminal activity of [[The Royale Flush]], but prefers to let [[The Maestro]] manage that aspect.
