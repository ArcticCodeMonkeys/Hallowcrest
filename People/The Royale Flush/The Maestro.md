The Maestro was a powerful mob boss of a group known as the Uptown Kings in [[The Capital]]. He studied classical music theory in bard college and was a conductor for the Grand Empirical Orchestra. The Maestro was revered as a magnificent orchestrator, but not just in music. He is also the mastermind of the greatest heist of all time known as the Royal Gala Gambit, in which he and a small crew successfully stole over 122,000 gold pieces worth of crown jewels from [[The Capital]]. He used this capital to evade capture, abandoning the Uptown Kings and moved to [[Hallowcrest]], where he began to set up [[Fortune's Favor]] before meeting [[Lady Luck]], who had a similar plan.

The two decided on a joint venture, turning [[Fortune's Favor]] into the largest casino in the world. The Maestro manages the criminal side of the business, with his official role being Security and Wealth Manager. The Maestro scouts new recruits, plans heists, and handles loose ends for [[The Royale Flush]] while [[Lady Luck]] focuses on managing the casino floor. He does not personally step in very often as he spends most of his time in his office in [[Fortune's Favor]], making the only chances to meet him during his bi-annual orchestral performances at [[Locations/Hideouts/Aurora College|Aurora College]], or when being recruited. If you meet him in any other circumstance, you're likely already dead.

The Maestro has successfully concealed his identity up until now. No one knows his true name or face, and he prefers to keep it that way. He is extremely intelligent, collected, and well planned. He is also an extreme perfectionist, and will stop at nothing, including murder, to make sure everything goes as planned.
