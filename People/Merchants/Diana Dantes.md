Tiefling Woman. Owner of [[Fondantes Inferno]]. Once part of a cult of Asmodeus, she escaped and left that life behind. She now spends her time partaking in her passion: baking. She has adopted 5 kids from the orphanage, who help her out by running around [[Grand Plaza]] and cutely advertising her store. She takes her kids to [[Arcanedy]] often, handing out free samples much to the silent discontent of [[William Winkerton]]. 

When her bakery is closed, she helps out at the [[House of Devotion]].
