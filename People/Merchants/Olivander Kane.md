Owner of the [[Spice Of Life]], he is a calm and well meaning human male who has a deep admiration for tea, aromas, and flowers. He harvests spices and flowers from his garden in the back of his shop, some of which have magical properties. Some wildlife have been eating some of the fruits of his garden, causing him a loss in profits and inflicting the wildlife with odd magical properties.
