Owner of [[The Booby Trap]]. Secretly a Rakshasa. [[Sylvester Iblis]] uses the brothel as a scheme to get new warlocks of Asmodeus and to acquire souls, broker deals, and make money. He has no interest in the Grand Game. Also acts as a lawyer for some of the most influential people in the city. Its a well kept secret, but he is able to skirt around Zone of Truth spells due to being a Rakshasa.
