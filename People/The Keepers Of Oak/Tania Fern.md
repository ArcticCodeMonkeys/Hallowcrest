![[Tania Fern.jpg]]Nature Cleric, leader of [[The Keepers of Oak]]. Engaged to [[Andrew Bramblenest]]. Tania came to [[Hallowcrest]] with [[Gestalt Hallowbrand]], on her own mission to scout the terrain and look for any advantages as well as tend to the wounded. She was the first to ascend [[Searclaw]]'s rest and discovered [[Ne're Darke]]'s plan to enslave [[Daurgothoth]]. Highly against this, she warned the [[Sovereign Guard]] and called upon the druids of her conclave as well as any others to join her in ensuring that [[Daurgothoth]] and no other creature is enslaved by [[Ne're Darke]], forming a group which eventually became [[The Keepers of Oak]]. After [[The Battle of Ash and Ember]], the group stayed in Hallowcrest to ensure that the new city would not harm the beautiful ecosystem around it. Tania is very wise and intelligent, spending most of her time advising to people around her and reading. She has a very determined demeanor, more focused on her goals than most of the people around her, though her goals usually imply protecting people around her. 

Sister of [[Andressa Fern]] and [[Emma Fern]]
