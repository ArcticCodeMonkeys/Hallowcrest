Enya remains at [[The Other Bar]] in the back, and prefers to simply spend her time playing cards with the other members of [[The Menagerie]]. When a big heist is coming up, Enya will use magic to disguise herself as a beautiful noble woman and curse certain key targets or bestow them with a disease. She also performs Scrying services, which are highly illegal.
