Jynx was born in [[Hallowcrest]]. In a spur of Wild Magic, her birth caused a massive explosion, killing both her parents, the midwife, and destroying an entire city block. She never got a real name, a jinx was what she was called at the orphanage, as bad luck and tragedy seemed to follow wherever she went. She spun it as her name; Jynx.

She knew [[Roach]] from the orphanage, and when he started [[The Menagerie]] she was one of the first to join. Roach saw her use as a mercenary, using her outbursts as a distraction and her aura of bad luck to make kills look like accidents. 

Jynx is upbeat and curious, a little bit fucked up, but overall pretty nice. She as mostly accepted her bad luck thanks to [[Roach]] and sees it as a gift more than a curse now-a-days. She still wishes that she could have a normal life with a small family instead, but accepts that this is her life.
