Roach was born in Hallowcrest, his existence a stain. His mother was human, but had been assaulted by a goblin from [[Shamblewood]]. His existence was a constant reminder of the traumatic event, and she could not bear to care for him. He was left at the orphanage where he gained the name Roach, partly due to his secluded nature, prefering to be alone in the dark, and partly due to his obsession with the roaches infesting the orphanage.

Even among the misfits of the orphanage, Roach was an outcast. He called the bugs his only friends, as the children were afraid of them too; at least they had that in common. He spent most of his time talking to them, playing make believe, and reading stories. Roach dreamed of an escape, and from his story books, he began to imagine escape plans, schemes to start a new life, and a family who truely cared about him.

As Roach turned 16, he was forced to leave the orphanage and live on his own. Of course, he had not found his family yet, but he decided that he would make it himself. His whole live he lived as an outcast; a misfit among misfits. He liked it, but wanted to share it with someone. So he started a group called [[The Menagerie]]. The group would be a family for the outcasts of the world, and together they would make a name for themselves. People would be forced to recognize them and treat them as equals, not despite their differences, but for their differences.

Roach is an extremely intelligent individual, skilled in creating plans and scouting. He performs most of the organizational tasks for [[The Menagerie]], planning deals, heists, and strikes. He also uses his army of roaches as spies, using Speak with Animals to have them act as 'bugs'.
