A Charismatic Drow Swordsman, he wears elegant and fine clothing, with many frills, and wields his signature Emerald Rapier, [[Emerald Elegance]]. He believes that drow are a superior race, which is why he restricts the faction to mainly drow, and why he has a large collection of powerful and exotic creatures captured in the main hideout.

Uses many disguises to spy, and has some aliases to get new connections. He tries to keep his true identity secret, keeping it only for meetings in which he is representing all of [[Ne're Darke]]. Otherwise, he will resort to using one of his many aliases.

He is beginning a movement among the drow to seek representation in the Matriarchal society that the drow live in. He is incredibly strong and the defacto leader of [[Ne're Darke]] as a whole, not just in [[Hallowcrest]]. He hopes that by acquiring [[Hallowcrest]] under his control, he can spread the drow influence to the surface world. He is a much more democratic drow than those "brutish scavengers" and beleives that the biggest mistake by [[Ne're Darke]]
