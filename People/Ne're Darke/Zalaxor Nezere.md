A Charismatic Drow Swordsman, he wears elegant and fine clothing, with many frills, and wields his signature Emerald Rapier, [[Emerald Elegance]]. He believes that drow are a superior race, which is why he restricts the faction to mainly drow, and why he has a large collection of powerful and exotic creatures captured in the main hideout.

Uses many disguises to spy, and has some aliases to get new connections. He tries to keep his true identity secret, keeping it only for meetings in which he is representing all of [[Ne're Darke]]. Otherwise, he will resort to using one of his many aliases.

 He is the defacto leader of [[Ne're Darke]] as a whole, not just in [[Hallowcrest]] due to his revolutionary ideologies and incredible power and motivation. He hopes that by acquiring [[Hallowcrest]] under his control, he can spread the drow influence to the surface world and potentially become Lloth's [[Chosen]]. He is a much more democratic drow than those "brutish scavengers" and believes that the biggest mistake by [[Ne're D. Darke]] was trying to take [[Astell]] by force. His goals are to simply create the best lives possible for the drow, as he sees the benefits to living in a society and building alliances, something which [[Ne're D. Darke]] did not. 

He also likes to collect creatures, as despite seeing drow as the superior race, he says "Each and every creature has its quirks and oddities. They are entertaining little things; most welcome." He has a large collection of creatures, stored under his alias [[Zane Alyss]] who more outwardly shares his opinions on the creatures and displays them at the [[Tropic Tent]].
