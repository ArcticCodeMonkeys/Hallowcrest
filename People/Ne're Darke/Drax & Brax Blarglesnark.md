![[Drax and Brax Blarglesnark.jpg]]
Drow enforcer twins of [[Ne're Darke]].

Coming from the Underdark, Drax and Brax grew up constantly fighting. They knew battle from the age of just 4, and spent most of their time practicing against the monsters of the depths. Eventually, they become mercenaries for [[Ne're Darke]] and worked closely with [[Ne're Darke]] for a few years before his death. 

Pre-occupied with a distraction operation in [[The Capital]], the two were not present for [[The Battle of Ash and Ember]], but wish they were and swear that they would have been able to turn the tides.

They are very quiet and mysterious. Not much is known about them other than that they get the job done, no questions asked. Some believe their strength to be supernatural, this theory aided by their pitch black eyes and sheet white irises. Others say it is an adaptation of the harsh life of the underdark.

Currently, they work for [[Zalaxor Nezere]].
