
An alias for [[Dr. Edward Monroe]], which he uses to commit murders and kidnappings throughout the city, along with his apprentices, in order to experiment on them and attempt to create the Perfect Lifeform.

As The Sculptor, he collects people and creatures for analysis and experimentation, opting to add mechanical enhancements and grafting two or more different creatures together, often from different species, to conjoin the strengths of each and remove their weaknesses.

Apprentices:

- [[Elzar the Magnificent]] - a master of the mind. Acts as an informant in the [[Tropic Tent]] in order to aquire rare specimens.
- [[Panambre Stark]] - a master of undeath. Acts quietly in the shadows and looks to acquire bodies from [[The Crypts]] that are very powerful, such as that of [[Gestalt Hallowbrand]]
- [[Meat]] - one of The Sculptor's very first successful experiments. Meat is [[Dr. Edward Monroe]]'s brother.
