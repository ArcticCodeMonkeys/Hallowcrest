Necromancer of the [[Society of Higher Minds]], but does not make public appearances. Works for [[Dr. Edward Monroe]], granting him specimens to experiment on without harming real people. He has become increasingly upset with the specimens though, as they are much too different from a real person. 

Ex-Husband of [[Eliza Stark]] and father of [[Gwyn Stark]]. Panambre Stark was once a simple wizard, at least to most who knew him including his family. It was actually in his childhood that he became obsessed with the dead. Both of his parents died when he was just 9 years old, murdered in front of him in the dead of night. Instead of calling the guard, he simply sat there with them, staring at them, traumatized. He cleaned them up, dressed them, and brought them home in his wagon, placing them at the dinner table. 4 days later, the guard came to investigate after some missing persons reports and found the rotting and decaying bodies of his parents, Panambre sitting between them casually eating his breakfast.

At the orphanage, Panambre was constantly surrounded with death. The conditions were not the best, leading to many children dying from sickness, starvation, or suicide. He became accustomed to it. In his tumultuous life, death was the only constant. It was a peace and a constant presence for him. As such, he became quite fond of the dead, seeing them as his only friends. He became a mortician in [[The Capital]], where he met his co-worker [[Dr. Edward Monroe]] and his wife [[Eliza Stark]] with whom he had a child, [[Gwyn Stark]]. [[Eliza Stark]] and Panambre divorced after she found him caressing a severed woman's hand, alerting the guard and forcing Panambre into a recluse lifestyle.
