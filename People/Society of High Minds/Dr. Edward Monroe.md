Advanced Surgeon, Mortician, and Inventor. Enlightened One in the [[Society of Higher Minds]]. 

Dr. Monroe has a contract with the [[Sovereign Guard]] as a CSI and autopsy performer for murders within the city. 

He is openly very interested in progress, invention, and uncovering the secrets of humanity. He hopes that through his life's work and studies, he can improve the lives of the people of Hallowcrest, and the rest of the world. 

In [[The Auditorium]], Dr. Monroe sells different inventions which interface with the human body. He calls these inventions "Mods". These take the form of artificial eyes, prosthetics, and a small device which can transfuse blood. He is seen helping the sick and injured through his inventions, and received a large grant and prize last year from the Capital in recognition for his contributions to his fields. 

Dr. Monroe goes by an alias; [[The Sculptor]]. He uses this alias to discuss with his apprentices and commit murders and kidnappings throughout the city. Together, [[The Sculptor]] and his apprentices perform experiments on their victims in the name of progress and evolution, seeking to create the Perfect Lifeform. They do not see their actions as wrong, but know they are shunned by the public. They believe that science and progress are objectively the most important aspect of humanity, and that no sacrifice or deed is too large in the name of science.

As a child. Dr. Monroe was born with heavy deficiencies and numerous genetic defects, including a weak heart and maformed hands. His mother did not want him and so his father took care of him, if you could say that. Dr. Monroe spent his life for the most part ignored by his father and looked down upon by him. He would constantly belittle him for not being able to do things and for getting tired and sick often. Edward couldn't go outside as he was too prone to disease and illness, so he remained inside and learned the art of artifice. His first invention, at the age of 10, a set of mechanical hands to replace that of his own. He performed his own amputation and successfully replaced them, before moving onto his life support system he called his Regulatory Apparatus. It helps pump and filter his blood for disease and illnesses, regulate body temperature, and enhance breathing.  He finished this device by the time he was 13 and showed it to his father. A mess of steam, tubes and glowing parts, his father questioned where he acquired the material. Admitting he had been taking money to purchase them, his father beat him until his apparatus was nearly destroyed, all the while belittling him for his frailty and imperfections. Injured and afraid, Edward stepped out into the streets and ran away, barely kept alive by what was left of his apparatus.

He found an abandoned building which he began to live in, selling little trinkets and inventions to people on the streets in order to feed himself and repair his apparatus. His inventions quickly caught on, becoming a well known local, and so he instituted the Society of Higher Minds, a place for great minds and inventors to share and sell their inventions.
