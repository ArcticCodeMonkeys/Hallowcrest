Searclaw and [[Gestalt Hallowbrand]] met in the [[Spine of the North]], where [[Gestalt Hallowbrand]] was looking for a powerful weapon known as [[Ashbringer]]. Searclaw had [[Ashbringer]] in his hoard, and the two met in a vicious battle. [[Gestalt Hallowbrand]] was able to overpower Searclaw, and with [[Ashbringer]] over his head, chose to spare him instead, granting him Searclaw's gratitude and respect. Years later, in a nearby town, a group of [[Ne're Darke]] mercenaries were terrorizing the people there, who often gave Searclaw tributes in return for his protection. Searclaw came to their aid, but so did [[Gestalt Hallowbrand]], leading the two to meet again. After their fight together against [[Ne're Darke]], [[Searclaw]] offered to travel with [[Gestalt Hallowbrand]], aiding him in his efforts against [[Ne're Darke]] and training along side him.

Along their adventures, the two became incredibly close friends. They spend every waking and unwaking hour together for over 40 years, forming a [[Soul Bond]].

In [[The Battle of Ash and Ember]], [[Gestalt Hallowbrand]] was killed, leading [[Searclaw]] to become incredibly reclusive and depressed. He retreated into the nearby volcanic mountain, which was then dubbed [[Searclaw's Rest]], where he has remained for the last 27 years. He only leaves once per year, on the anniversary of [[The Battle of Ash and Ember]], [[Victory Day]]. 

Searclaw is well respected and loved by the people of [[Hallowcrest]], especially by the group known as [[Ember's Creed]]. They are a group / religion dedicated to his worship and praise. Worshipers of [[Searclaw]] are sometimes bestowed great powers by him and worship him for his power, strength, and heroics.
