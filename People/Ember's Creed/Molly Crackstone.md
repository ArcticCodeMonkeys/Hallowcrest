Cleric of [[Ember's Creed]]. She spends most of her time at the [[Hearthstone]]. %%CENSOR%%She has a crush on [[Rebecca Fletcher]], who is just next door at [[Fletcher's Fletchery]]. %%/CENSOR%%

%%CENSOR%%
Molly's father was a dragonborn and her mother was a human, presenting itself through her human appearance with some scales along her shoulders, arms and face, as well as her horns. Her father was one of the first people to attempt to reach out to [[Searclaw]] after [[The Battle of Ash and Ember]] and a founding member of [[Ember's Creed]]. He died of sickness when Molly was young, and her mother followed soon after. She grew up in the orphanage, but met [[Ashley Hart]] when she was around 16. The two created [[Hearthstone]], which was the first church of [[Ember's Creed]] within [[Hallowcrest]], as the others were more monastic temples on [[Searclaw's Rest]].
%%/CENSOR%%

Molly is quiet and reserved, but very caring and thoughtful. She admires [[Searclaw]] for his willingness to help people, and sees that as a driving factor in her beliefs and how she tries to live her life. She takes care of [[Prometheus Wick]] with [[Ashley Hart]] as well as any who come seeking aid.
