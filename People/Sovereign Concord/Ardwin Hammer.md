Half Orc Lieutenant of the [[Sovereign Guard]]. Ardwin and his brother [[Quinn Hammer]] both lived in the Capital for most of their lives. When they turned 18, Ardwin enlisted in the [[Sovereign Guard]] while his brother turned to a life of crime. Ardwin spent most of his life chasing after his brother, trying to convince him to stop and join him in the [[Sovereign Guard]]. Due to their Half Orc lineage, they were often judged or mistreated. [[Quinn Hammer]] eventually heard word of a new organization forming in [[Hallowcrest]], known as [[The Menagerie]], which is a group for misfits and outcasts to make a new life. He moved to [[Hallowcrest]] and so Ardwin requested a transfer to follow.

In Hallowcrest, Ardwin spends his time asking around about his brother, paying people to look out for him and making sure his criminal partners don't turn on him. [[Quinn Hammer]] makes this hard though, and has made it clear that he wants nothing to do with Ardwin.
