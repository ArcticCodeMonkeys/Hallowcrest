Quest Giver for the [[Sovereign Concord]] and Lieutenant of the [[Sovereign Guard]]. She is quite young for a Lieutenant, being only 28. She joined the [[Sovereign Guard]] when she was 16 in [[The Capital]] and proved to be quite a capable leader. She is very brave and unyielding, but also caring and selfless. She was appointed to [[Hallowcrest]] by [[Aurora Stellaris]] who learned about her from [[Gestalt Hallowbrand]]'s journal. Apparently, she served under him for a few years and he noted that she showed extreme promise in a higher position. In [[Hallowcrest]], she has been one of the best Lieutenants, maybe beside [[Ardwin Hammer]]. She has also become good friends with [[Elowyn Hallowbrand]], whom she had been advising with up until he recently decided to take decisions into his own hands to show leadership to his people. The two are gossiped about often, with many members of the public and even [[Aurora Stellaris]] seeing them as a possible couple. The two do not see each other this way though, and swear they are just good friends.
