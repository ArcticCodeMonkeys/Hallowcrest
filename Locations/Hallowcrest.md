Welcome to Hallowcrest! A town of hope, unity, courage, and innovation with a rich history, promising future, and political intrigue. Hallowcrest was founded in 1276 SE (Soverign Era) in the aftermath of the [[The Battle of Ash and Ember]]. The city was named after Gestalt Hallowbrand, a mighty warchief and respected army general from the Capital, who unfortunately perished in the battle. Building the city in his name, Aurora Costella, a powerful mage and researcher of the stars ruled over the city, bearing Hallowbrand's child Gestalt Stellaris. In 1324 SE, as Gestalt came of age, he become the new lord of Hallowcrest, and remains so to this day, while Aurora Costella established a magic school for bards, wizards, and scholars alike to study magic and the cosmos, named Aurora College.

The city is split into 6 major districts:

## The Lion's Den:
![[Pasted image 20241202184510.png]]The city's guild district and the first district to be seen entering through the main gates, filled with many different guilds, taverns, and criminal organizations. A point of key interest is Victory Hill, a memorial to the fallen soldiers of the Battle of Ash and Ember.

### The Docks:
The Port District, filled with taverns, docks, and ships galore. A point of key interest is Fortune's Favor, a large Casino run by the Royale Flush.
### Grand Plaza:
The Residential District, populated by the majority of Hallowcrest, this district is mostly home to the middle and lower class, containing houses and inns. A point of key interest is The Twin Protectors, a massive statue in the middle of the main square depicting Gestalt Hallowcrest upon his Wyvern, Searclaw.
### Silverside:
The trades district, located centrally and close to Grand Plaza; it's the perfect place to stock up on anything you need. Here you can buy adventuring gear, mundane items, stocks and treasuries, and even magic items. A point of key interest is Aurora College, a magical school on the edge of the Silverside and High Grounds where wizards and bards go to learn magic and study the stars.

### High Grounds:
An Upper-Class District, home to the rich elite and nobility of Hallowcrest and positioned on an elevated hill. A point of key interest is Smokepowder Keep, home to Lord Gestalt Stellaris and the Crestguard.

### The Crypts:
A large underground catacomb filled with the graves and tombs of many great warriors, including that of Gestalt Hallowbrand the founder of Hallowcrest. It also acts as a hideout for criminal organizations.
