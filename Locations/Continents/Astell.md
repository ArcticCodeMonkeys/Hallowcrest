The Material Plane. Not fully explored or mapped yet. There are 3 main continents so far:

[[Golden Plains]], the most populated area, with large fields of grain, located to the west.

[[Spine of the North]], a large mountainous and cold area, located to the north

[[Silver Coast]], a rocky and forested area newly discovered, located to the east.

In the middle of all 3 is a large ocean known as [[The Great Sea]].

Underneath it all is the [[Underdark]], a large system of caverns inhabited by drow and mindflayers.
