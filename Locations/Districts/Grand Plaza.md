The trades district, located centrally and close to Silverside; it's the perfect place to stock up on anything you need. Here you can buy adventuring gear, mundane items, stocks and treasuries, and even magic items. A point of key interest is The [[Twin Protectors]], a massive statue in the middle of the main square depicting [[Gestalt Hallowbrand]] upon his Wyvern, [[Searclaw]].
