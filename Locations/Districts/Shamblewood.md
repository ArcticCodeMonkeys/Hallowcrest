The large forest of mostly willow and oak trees to the South of [[Hallowcrest]], home to [[The Tree of Life]] and the enclave for [[The Keepers of Oak]]. While technically governed by [[Hallowcrest]], the people of [[Shamblewood]] mostly govern themselves and act almost like a neighboring nation, with good relations to the [[Sovereign Concord]]. They are not technically a district of [[Hallowcrest]], but is often referred to as one.

Shamblewood expands very far into the south, into an area not yet mapped. There are many different monsters within the woods so not many have ventured too far south.
