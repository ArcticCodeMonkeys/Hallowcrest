A brothel, tavern, and inn located in [[The Lion's Den]] %%CENSOR%%run by [[Sylvester Iblis]]. The workers of the brothel are all secretly Succubae and Incubi, and the owner is a Rakshasa. [[Sylvester Iblis]] uses the brothel as a scheme to get new warlocks of Asmodeus and to acquire souls, broker deals, and make money. He has no interest in the Grand Game.%%/CENSOR%%
