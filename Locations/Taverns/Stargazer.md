The giant observatory near the top of [[Locations/Hideouts/Aurora College|Aurora College]] in [[Silverside]], used to study the stars and attempt to master divination magic. [[Aurora Stellaris]] spends most of her time here, currently conducting an experiment to attempt to decern what stars are made out of. She hopes that a better understanding of how the stars work will better improve her magic.

At night, the Stargazer is converted into a bar for the students of [[Locations/Hideouts/Aurora College|Aurora College]],  tended to by [[Neil Degassi]]
%%/CENSOR%%
