Adventurer's Guild and Tavern for adventurers in [[Silverside]], owned and tended by [[Sir Finley Steinhart]]. The building is filled with severed monster heads as trophies, pictures of different adventuring groups, mostly of [[Sir Finley Steinhart]] himself, and assorted oddities he collected throughout his trip. The [[Fucked Up Four]] frequent to drink and pick up new quests.

[[Sir Finley Steinhart ]]occasionally organizes Wild Hunts, events where a large group of adventurers will go out into the wilderness to hunt down and slay a specific monster. Each member gets a trophy from the monster and a tale to tell.

They also organize the [[Contest of Champions]], an annual tournament where adventurers compete for a series of grand prizes. There is a small entry fee which partially funds the tournament, and the rest is personally funded from [[Sir Finley Steinhart]]. The grand prize is often a powerful magic item, as well as the incidental spectating noble reaching out to the champions for a special contract.
