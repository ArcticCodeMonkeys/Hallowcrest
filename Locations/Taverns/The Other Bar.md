
Built shortly after [[The Rusty Tankard]] by [[Bob Boblin]], advertised as an alternative with worse but cheaper drinks and lodging for those who were kicked out of the neighboring establishment. The tavern is quite rowdy and fights break out multiple times a night, usually involving a member of [[The Menagerie]], who spend a lot of their time here and use the back room as a base of operations.
