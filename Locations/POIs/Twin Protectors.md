A large bridge connecting [[Grand Plaza]], [[Silverside]], [[High Grounds]] and [[The Lion's Den]], crossing the [[The Vive]]. The bridge is notably towered by a large statue of [[Gestalt Hallowbrand]] atop [[Searclaw]], exhaling a breath of flame towards the bridge in a victorious pose, built in commemoration of their heroics towards the yet founded [[Hallowcrest]].


District: [[Grand Plaza]]
