A large hill in [[The Lion's Den]] marking the first flag planted in [[Hallowcrest]] after [[The Battle of Ash and Ember]] was won. Here, many of the last bodies of the battle were buried as time and space permitted. During the battle, bodies were placed in what became [[The Crypts]] due to a lack of space or time, but after the battle and as [[Hallowcrest]] was being established, they needed to pave over [[The Crypts]] as so [[Victory Hill]] become a graveyard and monument to all of the dead from [[The Battle of Ash and Ember]]. Other heroic members of the [[Sovereign Guard]] have also been buried here, including those who both did and did not participate in [[The Battle of Ash and Ember]].
