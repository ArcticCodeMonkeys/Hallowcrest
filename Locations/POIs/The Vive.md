The large artificial canal / river running through [[Hallowcrest]]. Initially created by a large line of Smokepowder explosives being set off in the front lines of [[The Battle of Ash and Ember]] by [[Gestalt Hallowbrand]], the long crater was built upon, leveled, and expanded into the ocean and neighboring lakes to create an artificial river. The river itself is only about 20 meters wide, so large boats are not allowed on them, but smaller canoes and fishing vessels are allowed passage. It is used as the primary water source for [[Hallowcrest]], passing through a treatment plant as it enters the city to be purified and remove the salt and debris, while still permitting wildlife to flow through.
