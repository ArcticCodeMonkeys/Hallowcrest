A large set of farm land part of the original settlements of the miners for [[Crackrock Quarry]]. This farm is all that is left of that settlement, and is managed by [[Grumsh Torval]], a Half-Orc farmer. The land is enchanted by [[The Keepers of Oak]] to boost its productivity to ensure that the land can feed the city.


District: [[Shamblewood]]
