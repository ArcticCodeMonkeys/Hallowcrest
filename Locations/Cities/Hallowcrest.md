Welcome to Hallowcrest! A town of hope, unity, courage, and innovation with a rich history, promising future, and political intrigue. Hallowcrest was founded in 1246 SE (Sovereign Era) in the aftermath of the [[The Battle of Ash and Ember]]. The city was named after [[Gestalt Hallowbrand]], a mighty war chief and respected army general from [[The Capital]], who unfortunately perished in the battle. Building the city in his name, [[Aurora Stellaris]], a powerful mage and researcher of the stars ruled over the city, bearing Hallowbrand's twin children [[Elowyn Hallowbrand]], and [[Marcus Stellaris]]. In 1263 SE, as [[Elowyn Hallowbrand]] came of age, he become the new lord of Hallowcrest, and remains so to this day, while [[Aurora Stellaris]] established a magic school for bards, wizards, and scholars alike to study magic and the cosmos, named [[Locations/Hideouts/Aurora College|Aurora College]], soon taken over by [[Marcus Stellaris]]. 

The city is split into 6 major districts:

## [[The Lion's Den]]:
![[The Lion's Den.png]]

The city's guild district and the first district to be seen entering through the main gates, filled with many different guilds, taverns, and criminal organizations. A point of key interest is [[Victory Hill]], a memorial to the fallen soldiers of [[The Battle of Ash and Ember]].

### [[The Docks]]:
![[The Docks.jpg]]

The Port District, filled with taverns, docks, and ships galore. A point of key interest is [[Fortune's Favor]], a large Casino run by [[the Royale Flush]].

### [[Silverside]]:
![[Silverside.jpg]]

The Residential District, populated by the majority of [[Hallowcrest]], this district is mostly home to the middle and lower class, containing houses and inns.. A point of key interest is [[Locations/Hideouts/Aurora College|Aurora College]], a magical school on the edge of the [[Silverside]] and [[High Grounds]] where wizards and bards go to learn magic and study the stars.
### [[Grand Plaza]]:
![[Grand Plaza.jpg]]

The trades district, located centrally and close to [[Silverside]]; it's the perfect place to stock up on anything you need. Here you can buy adventuring gear, mundane items, stocks and treasuries, and even magic items.  A point of key interest is The [[Twin Protectors]], a massive statue in the middle of the main square depicting [[Gestalt Hallowbrand]] upon his Wyvern, [[Searclaw]]

### [[High Grounds]]:
![[High Grounds.jpg]]

An Upper-Class District, home to the rich elite and nobility of [[Hallowcrest]] and positioned on an elevated hill. A point of key interest is [[Smokepowder Keep]], home to Lord [[Elowyn Hallowbrand]] and the [[Sovereign Guard]].

### [[The Crypts]]:
![[The Crypts.jpg]]

A large underground catacomb filled with the graves and tombs of many great warriors, including that of [[Gestalt Hallowbrand]] the founder of [[Hallowcrest]]. It also acts as a hideout for criminal organizations, namely [[Ne're Darke]], which is why it is highly discouraged to enter.

## [[Shamblewood]]

![[Shamblewood.jpg]]

The large forest of mostly willow and oak trees to the South of [[Hallowcrest]], home to [[The Tree of Life]] and the enclave for [[The Keepers of Oak]]. While technically governed by [[Hallowcrest]], the people of [[Shamblewood]] mostly govern themselves and act almost like a neighboring nation, with good relations to the [[Sovereign Concord]]. They are not technically a district of [[Hallowcrest]], but is often referred to as one.


## Entrances:

There are 3 entrances to the city, the Main Entrance being from the North Gate, while two others, the South Gate and the East gate lead out into the wilderness. [[The Docks]] are actually the most common entrance point however, since [[Hallowcrest]] has no neighboring cities.

## Layers:

Hallowcrest is commonly considered to have 'Layers' to the city. 

The deepest layer being the [[Underdark]]. It is not known by anyone other than the most elite [[Ne're Darke]] members how to get into the [[Underdark]] from [[Hallowcrest]], but it is believed that there is an entrance due to the never ending forces of Drow during [[The Battle of Ash and Ember]].

Above that is [[The Crypts]] built upon the remnants of a battlefield and a limestone quarry, [[Crackrock Quarry]].

The next layer is the sewers, in which most entrances to [[The Crypts]] are sealed but criminals know how to get between them.

The next layer is the street level, this is where the main city is located.

The final layer is [[High Grounds]], both geographically and structurally higher up than the rest of the city, built upon large walls and ramparts.
