A large college for bards, wizards, scribes, and more recently sorcerers and artificers. Located in [[Silverside]] near [[Victory Hill]], it was established in 1277 SE by [[Aurora Stellaris]].

See also [[Factions/Aurora College|Aurora College]] (Faction)

Key Figures:
- [[Aurora Stellaris]] - Founder
- [[Marcus Stellaris]] - Dean
%%CENSOR%%
- [[Octavius Aurelius]] - Professor, School of Divination
- [[Professor Thimblebang]] - Professor, School of Evocation, Head of Research
  %%/CENSOR%%
