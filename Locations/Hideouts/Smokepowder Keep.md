The Large Keep / Castle belonging to the Lord of [[Hallowcrest]], [[Elowyn Hallowbrand]]. It is located in [[High Grounds]] and home to the [[Sovereign Guard]]. Within is the [[Sealed Vault]] and beneath it is [[Ironwrought Prison]]. It is named after the large Smokepowder explosion set off by [[Gestalt Hallowbrand]] during the [[The Battle of Ash and Ember]], creating [[The Vive]] which flows by the keep.
