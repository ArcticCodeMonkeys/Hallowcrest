The bureaucratic organization controlling the laws and documentations of the city. Here is where taxes are filed, permits can be acquired, and city jobs can be given. 

The Magistrate also upkeeps the [[Sending Poles]], repairs roads and infrastructure, and set up the lamps throughout the city, which are light by Continual Flames.

They also represent the city in legal affairs, and hold trials against criminals and for civil disputes.

They fund the [[Lifeweavers]], [[Rainwalkers]], and [[Weaveseers]].

## Permits:

The following permits can be purchased, allowing specific permission and rights that not everyone has. A permit is a one-time purchase, but can be revoked or refused at any time if the Magistrate has just cause.

| Permit               | Cost                | Benefit                                                                                                                   |
| -------------------- | ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Adventurer's Permit  | 20gp                | Charges of Assault against Criminals, Brandishing of Weapons in a Safe Manner, and Unintentional Vandalism are all waved. |
| Merchant's Permit    | 50gp                | Permits a buisness to Sell Non-Magical Items                                                                              |
| Magical Store Permit | 100gp               | Permits a business Sell Magical Items to permitted individuals                                                            |
| Magician's Permit    | 15gp                | Permits a person to utilize magic in a safe and non illegal manner in public. Permits the purchasing of magical items.    |
| Builder's Permit     | 25gp                | Permits the construction or major renovation of a building.                                                               |
| Fishing Permit       | 10gp                | Permits fishing from the river or sea                                                                                     |
| Cart Permit          | 10gp + Driving Test | Permits the piloting of a carriage or cart                                                                                |
| Boat Permit          | 20gp                | Permits the piloting of a vessel or ship                                                                                  |
| Shipping Permit      | 50gp                | Permits a buisness to import and export goods through Hallowcrest                                                         |
