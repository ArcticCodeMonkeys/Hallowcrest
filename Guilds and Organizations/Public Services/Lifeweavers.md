A group of clerics and druids, funded by [[The Magistrate]] who arrive to provide medical assistance to the city. Citizens of Hallowcrest who register with [[The Magistrate]] can purchase Health Insurance to reduce or negate the cost of the Lifeweavers assistance, to certain degrees.

Services:

The Lifeweavers provide the following services, at the following costs. See Health Insurance plan-specific pricing with [[The Magistrate]]

| Spell               | Price  |
| ------------------- | ------ |
| Gentle Repose       | 25gp   |
| Lesser Restoration  | 60gp   |
| Prayer of Healing   | 45gp   |
| Remove Curse        | 150gp  |
| Revivify            | 450gp  |
| Death Ward          | 600gp  |
| Greater Restoration | 1250gp |
| Reincarnate         | 2000gp |
