The main city guard of [[Hallowcrest]], lead by [[Elowyn Hallowbrand]] and made of soldiers from the [[Sovereign Concord]]. 

Roles:
Commander - The leader of the guards, title held only by the Lord of Hallowcrest
Lieutenant - A leader of a platoon, 10+ years of experience
Sergeant - A leader of a small squad, 5+ years of experience
Solider - A basic member, with anywhere from 1-3 years experience
Recruit - A new member with < 3 months experience

Groupings:
Partner - Two guards supporting each other, the smallest permitted grouping of guards.
Squad - 4-9 Soldiers or Recruits, lead by 1-2 Sergeants or a Lieutenant.
Platoon - 10-20 Soliders or Recruits, containing 1-2 Sergeants and lead by a Lieutenant
