Levels: 3

The party arrives at [[Fellowship of the Frontier]] after a long journey from the capital. It is only mid day, but they are quite tired from the fight and the travel. When they arrive, they'll meet [[Sir Finley Steinhart]], who will boldly approach them

"AHA! New Adventurers I see! From the Capital! On contract from THE Aurora Stellaris? I wonder what type of business could be so important! You shall regale your odyssey, fending of Ne're Darke on the high seas over some drinks! "

He'll take the party over to the bar and have them tell the story. If he's impressed, he'll say so, and then ask

"Well, you are likely looking for a place to stay right? And I assume our coin purses are a bit light?"

"Well I have the job for you! You see, my friend, [[Dominic Hart]], he works for [[Fine Print]]. They are the company in charge of the paper here in Hallowcrest. He's not much for journalism though, mainly works on maps in fact, but he does manage the brand new printing press! A marvel of a creation from the [[Society of Higher Minds]]! Apologies I've gone on a tangent. I went to meet him at Vanessa's where we always meet after work and he never showed up. I went over to his house and the door was locked. He wasn't at his office and he never leaves for an expedition without at least telling me first. I'm worried something has happened to him. Last I saw him was yesterday morning. We got coffee from [[Spice Of Life]] over in [[Silverside]] and I had to depart host haste because... well.. I saw my ex-wife. Anyways I told him we'd meet at [[Vanessa's]]"
