
The party has given information to [[Ardwin Hammer]] regarding the location of a [[Ne're Darke]] hideout learned from an assassin during [[Family Ties]]. He spoke with [[Elowyn Hallowbrand]] and they've decided they need to get more information from this lead than simply taking them down right away. They want some informants on the inside so they can get close to / learn the identity of their leader, only known as Z. [[Ardwin Hammer]] himself cannot do this, as he is too well known and so he decides to check in on the party and see if they'd be willing to do this for him. The [[Sovereign Concord]] will pay them the going rate for Sergeants plus a bonus for the first mission and a big payout if they uncover the identity of Z.

Daily: 5 Crowns each
Infiltrate Ne're Darke: 150 Crowns each
Discover the Identity of Z: 1000 Dragons total

As for the process of infiltration, look to [[License to Kill]].
