The party can be directed towards [[The Auditorium]] as they are having a large expo in which, among many other things, [[Dr. Edward Monroe]] and [[Spark Pritchet]] will be unveiling their new invention, the [[Armor Up Amulet]] and [[Marcus Stellaris]] will be unveiling the [[Arcane Cuffs]].  Someone stopping by their house can simply direct them there by saying that they hear there will be a lot of interesting technology being unveiled along with magic items and advancements in medicine (that last part should hopefully spark [[Felicity]] to follow up). 

They can arrive to watch the display, arriving just at the end of the demonstration for the [[Arcane Cuffs]]. [[Dr. Edward Monroe]] and [[Spark Pritchet]] will take the stage, with Monroe being the speaker and [[Spark Pritchet]] demonstrating. They explain how the [[Armor Up Amulet]] works, by folding space around the armor, it can expand and warp around you in an instant. They do a brief demonstration. 

And finally [[Dr. Edward Monroe]] says that he has a surprise additional invention to show off. He calls it, the Ail-Mentor. He wheels out a large metal sarcophagus, filled with tubes, pipes, and glowing energy. He explains the purpose; frequent treatments within the Ail-Mentor can slowly cure any disease, curse, or ailment afflicting someone, removing the scarcity of clerics and their services. He asks the crowd if anyone is ill and is willing to test it out, but if not will go in himself. 

The machine powers on and begins glowing with a dim gentle light. The machine hums and whirls as steam pours out of it. Suddenly, a loud clank is heard, the machine shudders and nearly topples, and the steam turns to smoke. The dim light turns bright and a hose breaks of spraying hot steam everywhere. The glass on the front of the machine cracks, and then all at once, the machine combusts into a cloud of smoke, metal, and light. (4d6 lightning damage, 4d6 piercing, 20ft radius).

[[Dr. Edward Monroe]] looks quite injured, but he assures everyone he is fine. It seems his pride is hurt more than his body. He states that the machine needs more time and that like all things in science, requires experimentation. He ends with a quite "Failure is not the end. It is a means to perfection." Before retreating to his workshop in the back.

[[Elzar the Magnificent]] gets the atmosphere back up by saying "Apolgies, esteemed guests, we'll get this cleaned up right away. In the meantime, feel free to peruse the stalls or stay by the stage in wait for a wonderful puppet show, run by I, Elzar the Magnificent."
