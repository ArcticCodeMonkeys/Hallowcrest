They decided to go investigate the large explosion, alongside [[Elowyn Hallowbrand]]. [[Hannah Baker]] has entered [[Sealed Vault]] to go stop them from stealing [[The Ashen Horn]]. Unfortunately, she is gravely injured, and while stable, she cannot stop them from escaping.

On round 3, initiative 20, a loud horn roars from within the smoke, sending it pouring in every direction. Your organs shudder and feel as though they are going to burst. A whispering of draconic tongue can be heard in your mind, beating against your brain, trying to force its message through the language barrier. DC 20 Wisdom Save, on a failure, you are compelled to put down your weapons and stop fighting, unless necessary for survival. If you have a sufficient method to flee combat without danger, you must take it. Dragons automatically fail this save and can't legendary resist it, so Searclaw just immediately flies away. 


So, coming out from the smoke is three Firestarters, it is unclear which one has the horn, but they will all run in different directions. If the party can keep up with the chase, they can attempt to chase after them, though their movement speed is much faster (50) while burning. The one heading North (to the left) is the one with the horn. 

If caught, they will recite a speech before self igniteing and burning to ash.
"Hallowcrest will burn. Down to ash. And the old kings and soulless nobles and demented fucks will crumple and become the embers from which we stoke a new flame. All will bow before Searclaw. All will fear Searclaw. All will worship Searclaw. And embrace in Eternal Flame!"

He ignites in flame and begins laughing maniacally while screaming in pain.
