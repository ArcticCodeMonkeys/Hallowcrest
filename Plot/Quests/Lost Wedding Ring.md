[[Orvold Parks]] has lost his wedding ring and knows his wife will be livid if he cannot find it. He's asked a wizard at [[Locations/Hideouts/Aurora College|Aurora College]] to divine its location and they said it was in the Sewers near his villa. He has put out a job asking for someone to retrieve the ring from within the sewers and return it in pristine shape.

Within the sewers, a Ne're Darke thief stole the ring, but was killed by a Carrion Crawler. A trail of blood leads from near the sewer entrance to its lair, where the mostly consumed thief lays with the ring on his finger.

Payout: 3 Dragons
