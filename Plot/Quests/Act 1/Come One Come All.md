
The [[Tropic Tent]] is in town, and it wont stay for long. Until the end of next month, parades, fireworks, circuses and market stalls carrying exotic goods will be occupying the central port of [[Hallowcrest]]. The party can receive a flyer at their door, advertising these things along with games to win prizes curtesy of [[Society of Higher Minds]].

We can have some carnival games, circus, and a parade. The "Magnificent Menagerie" parade will occur on 29 Lathand as a last hurrah and will have [[Zane Alyss]] parading the animals he has captured around [[Hallowcrest]]. This parade will spark a lot of inter faction conflict, as [[The Keepers of Oak]] and [[The Menagerie]] will make a joint effort to set these animals free, while [[Sovereign Concord]] and [[Ne're Darke]] will both want to keep the peace and not let the animals free. [[The Sculptor]] wants to claim a few of these animals for himself to experiment on, as a few have some very rare and useful qualities for the perfect life form.
