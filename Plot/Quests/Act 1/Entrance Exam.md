
Recommended Level: 3

So you want to join [[Factions/Aurora College|Aurora College]] huh? Well its a tough program and not many get in, but lets see if you've got what it takes. The Entrance exam mimics the school year; its split into two halves: Knowledge and Casting. The key to passing the entrance exam is mastering these two aspects of spellcasting. 

First, a student must pay for the entrance exam fee of 25 gp. Each subsequent attempt increases the cost by another 25gp.

A character can begin studying for the entrance exam by spending at least 8 hours doing one or more of the following. A character can only benefit from each of the following study methods once, but can benefit from multiple at a time:


| Study Method                  | Knowledge Bonus | Casting Bonus |
| ----------------------------- | --------------- | ------------- |
| Reading                       | +1              | 0             |
| Practicing                    | 0               | +1            |
| Study with a friend / student | +2              | 0             |
| Duel with a friend / student  | 0               | +2            |
| Study under a professor       | +3              | +1            |
| Duel a professor              | 0               | +4            |
| Steal a copy of the exam      | +7              | -1            |
| Get a body double             | -1              | +7            |

The final two options require skill tests to attempt, and working with a student or professor requires a relationship to be built with them. Failing the final two methods will result in -3 Renown with Aurora College and a permanent ban from joining them.


The entrance exam first begins in the Written Knowledge portion, where students must answer questions about history, spell components, spell schools, and spell inventors. This portion is done in a large hall where each student must pass through a Zone of Truth to recite the following phrase:

"I do not intend to, nor will I make any attempt to cheat during this exam. I understand that any action which is considered cheating under the Aurora College Code of Conduct will result in my immediate failure and a lifetime ban from the college. I am not currently under the effects of any spell or magical effect besides the Zone of Truth I am currently within. I have not and will not coordinated with any other student, professor, or person within or outside the exam hall in order to convey information during the exam. If I witness cheating or know of any cheating, I will state it immediately."

There is also a professor using Detect Magic to ensure that no spells are active heading into the exam hall. Once within, Arcane Eyes patrol the lines of desks, surveying students and ensuring they are not cheating. Students have 2 hours and 30 minutes to complete the written portion of the exam.

The student must make a DC 18 History, Arcana, and Insight check. For each roll, add the Knowledge bonus as well as any cheating bonuses, and then note the difference of the roll from the DC. If the sum of the differences is greater than or equal to 0, the student passes this portion of the exam.


The second portion of the exam takes place in the Courtyard, where the Zone of Truth is repeated but the detect magic is not. 

The Student must exhibit their skill in Somatic, Verbal, and Material Components, culminating in a final Duel against a professor.

The Student must make a DC 18 Sleight of Hand, Performance, and Arcana check. For each roll, add the Casting bonus as well as any cheating bonuses, and then note the difference of the roll from the DC. If the sum of differences is greater than or equal to 0, the student passes this portion of the exam.

Finally, the Duel against the professor. Students are placed in a 30 foot radius circle with a professor opposite to them, each of them donning inhibiting bracers to make their spells deal no damage and instead push the target 5 feet for every 5 points of damage they deal. Students are not allowed to make weapon attacks except for those caused by a spell or magical effect. Students are allowed to use magic items. If a student can remain within the bounds of the circle for 5 rounds, or can push the professor out of bounds before then, they pass this portion of the exam. If they are able to push the professor out of bounds, they are automatically signed up for [[Mage Tower]] tryouts, provided they passed the other portions of the exam.

If a student passes each portion of the exam, they are allowed into Aurora College, and automatically gain 3 Renown. They also are given two of the three items below:

- [[Headband of the Scholar]]
- [[Starry Pendant]]
- [[Student's Notebook]]
