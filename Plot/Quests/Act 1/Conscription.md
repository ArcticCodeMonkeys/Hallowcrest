
A Behir comes trampling through the city and is stopped by [[Sovereign Guard]] just outside of the party's house. If they choose to join in, they can fight alongside [[Ardwin Hammer]], who will see their prowess and ask them to join the Sovereign Guard's new "City Protectors" program. They get martial training and practice fighting in return for protecting the city and helping out the sovereign guard against larger threats from time to time.

[[Ardwin Hammer]] uses a sending stone to call for someone saying "Heard you're in charge of investigations involving monstrosities and stuff. We'll we've got a monstrosity here alright. Yes its dead. Not sure, its like a blue dragon without wings." [[Dr. Edward Monroe]] shows up after about 20 mins, being the city's new coroner and investigator into deaths and crimes related to rare and obsure creatures. He seems to have quite the knowledge about them.
