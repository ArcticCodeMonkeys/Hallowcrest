
Recommended Level: Levels 3-4

[[Fortune's Favor]] seem like a lovely place? Wanna get to know the owners a bit better? Well we'd love to have you in the family, but first, you'll have to prove your loyalty. 

The party can meet [[People/The Royale Flush/Quinn Hammer|Quinn Hammer]] at [[Fortune's Favor]], serving as the bartender. He will greet them as friends and apologize for the fight during [[An Easy Quest]]. He promises the orb is in good hands and offers to pay the party the difference that [[Aurora Stellaris]] didn't pay them in return for them doing a job for him.
