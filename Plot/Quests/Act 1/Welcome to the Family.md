
Recommended Level: Levels 3-4

[[Fortune's Favor]] seem like a lovely place? Wanna get to know the owners a bit better? Well we'd love to have you in the family, but first, you'll have to prove your loyalty. 

The party can meet [[People/The Royale Flush/Quinn Hammer|Quinn Hammer]] at [[Fortune's Favor]], serving as the bartender. He will greet them as friends and apologize for the fight during [[An Easy Quest]]. He promises the orb is in good hands and offers to pay the party the difference that [[Aurora Stellaris]] didn't pay them in return for them doing a job for him. The job is simple, and [[People/The Royale Flush/Quinn Hammer|Quinn Hammer]] figures that since the party has already tangled up with [[Ne're Darke]] before, they'd be a perfect fit for the job. 

See [[Ne're Darke]] is planning to assassinate an influential Nobleman named [[Steven Vanderblit]], the leader of the [[Architect's Guild]] who recently raised guild prices which heavily cut into their profits. All the party needs to do is protect him, without him realizing that he is being protected. He is a very do-it-himself kinda guy, and no matter how much convincing he will attempt to speak with [[Ne're Darke]] if he hears they have an issue with his guild fees, which will inevitably result in his death. [[People/The Royale Flush/Quinn Hammer|Quinn Hammer]] says his boss has a vested interest inn [[Steven Vanderblit]] remaining the leader of the [[Architect's Guild]] and needs him protected. Foil [[Ne're Darke]]'s assassination by sending a message to them, while keeping your presence hidden from [[Steven Vanderblit]].

[[Steven Vanderblit]] lives in [[High Grounds]], and the party will have to stealthily follow him around town, and take turns watching him while he sleeps for the next 2d4 -1 days. For each day they follow him, they must have a plan for the day and the night on how to remain hidden. Up to two characters can make perception checks to watch out for a Ne're Darke assassin and up to two characters can make stealth checks to attempt to hide from both [[Steven Vanderblit]] and the [[Ne're Darke]] assassin.
