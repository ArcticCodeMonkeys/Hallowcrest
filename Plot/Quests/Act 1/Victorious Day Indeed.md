This is the final quest of act 1 and not really a full quest.

During [[Extra Eyes]] or just [[Victory Day]] in general, the party will have the opportunity to encounter / hear about all of the parallel events that happen today.

First, here is a timeline of what happens.

The city is all decorated up for Victory Day with candles along window sills, flowers strung over the streets, and large wreaths upon doors. The streets are bustling and filled with excitement and clamor as people exchange gifts and small treats. 

The festivities begin just before dawn, where a small group of the more religious type (mainly [[Ember's Creed]]) conduct their first pilgrimage by lantern light as they climb [[Searclaw's Rest]] in time for the sunrise. There is a large ceremony there known as the Dawnbreak, in which everyone gives thanks and offerings to Searclaw. The ceremony concludes as [[Searclaw]] emerges from his lair and takes to the skies, where he remains for the rest of the day. 

This year, those who participated in Dawnbreak get to use the Drakegliders created by [[Society of Higher Minds]] to glide back down into the city. 

The first part of the day is dedicated to celebration, as people will walk down the streets, hand out gifts and treats, and play music. 

Things to do:
Canal Rides through [[The Vive]]
Create Floating Lanterns
Food Installations
Band Performances (Stage 1 at the Docks, Stage 2 in Grand Plaza, Stage 3 in High Grounds, Stage 4 at the Auditorium)

At noon, the Lord of Hallowcrest, [[Elowyn Hallowbrand]] will come to Stage 3 for a speech in thanks to Searclaw and in memory of those who fought for the city.

"Citizens of Hallowcrest, thank you for gathering here today. I hope you have enjoyed the festivities we and our lovely vendors and merchants have set up for you today. The day is just getting started, but before we continue with our festivities, I would like to remind you all what we are celebrating. Today is Victory Day. We call it that because it is the day in which we took back control from our oppressors. We concurred evil. We stood for justice and we triumphed. Our great protector Searclaw played a large part in that. And as he circles the sky above us now, then too he protected us from great threats." He pauses to allow the crowd to erupt into applause. Searclaw lets out an exhaling of flame in the sky in response.

"Let us also remember those who we lost. The brave and heroic members of the Sovereign Guard who fought for our freedoms and are sorely missed. Their sacrifice is what made this city. If you please, a moment of silence for my father, Gestalt Hallowbrand, and the thousands of other Sovereign Guard that we lost, as well as those who are still with us today."

As the moment of silence sits, a large explosion is heard in the background and a thick black cloud of smoke can be seen arising from [[Smokepowder Keep]].

The silence lingers for a few moments, people staring in fear, a sinking feeling covering the crowd. A few murmurs break out and then suddenly the entire crowd is discussing what has happened.

[[Elowyn Hallowbrand]] looks a bit unsure and worried, looking to the Sovereign Guard to his sides. Most of them rush off, led by [[Hannah Baker]].

"Uhm... Do not worry. We have the situation under control. My apologies."

The murmurs quiet slightly but do not die. Searclaw pivots course overhead to investigate the explosion.

"See, even Searclaw is on the case. There is no need to panic. Where was I? Oh yes. A great respect to those who fought in the Battle of Ash and Ember"

The murmurs finally settle

"I encourage all of you to go to Victory Hill at some point today to pay your respects to the fallen. Unfortunately as many of you know, we have been having some safety issues within the Crypts, so the Tomb of the Fallen Soldiers walking tour is cancelled this year. But, we will still have plenty of events as the day goes on. The parade will be starting momentarily, and tonight we have the releasing of the Floating Lanterns and the Sunset atop Searclaw's Rest. [[Ember's Creed]] will be hosting a Duskfall ceremony at 8pm at their Monastary. All are welcome. And [[Society of Higher Minds]] has created a new invention for us, the Drakewing they call it. A form of gliding wings which you can place upon your back to glide from the top of Searclaw's Rest back down into the city. We ask that you return these Gliders to [[The Auditorium]] or the nearest Sovereign Guard Post or Magistrate to you when you are finished. That being said, I won't bore you anymore. Let the parade begin."



So the parade begins, a bit of a shaky start as everyone's nerves are still high, but the festivities and music soon take over and clear up any tension. The Parade path heads east along the docks heading north, then turns back to the west to pass through Grand Plaza, it crosses over the Twin Protectors and loops around the northern part of Silverside to pass by Aurora College. The Parade pauses at Victory Hill, where the floats and dancers solemnly walk by with quiet and somber music playing, and only upon turning back upon the main streets does the music pick back up and the parade along with it. It follows [[The Vive]] as Gondalas join in on the parade. The parade ends at Smokepowder Keep, but has to end abruptly as the entire area is coordained off by Sovereign Guards. Keen eyes can see a massive hole blown into the ground around the entrance to [[Ironwrought Prison]]. The ground is scorched and dirt and rock is flung everywhere. 


### What Happened?

So, technically 3 things happened all at once.

First off, around 11am, the Sovereign Guard, lead by [[Jessie Faux]] and [[Ardwin Hammer]] went to meet with [[The Keepers of Oak]] to collect [[The Mindrend Crown]]. [[The Keepers of Oak]] hate [[The Mindrend Crown]] and had been keeping it this entire time within [[The Tree of Life]]. They didn't trust anyone, not even the [[Sovereign Concord]] to keep it safe. However, after the recent attack by [[Ember's Creed]], they no longer felt that they were equipped to keep it safe. So, they chose the faction whom they trusted the most to keep it safe, and arranged a pickup by the [[Sovereign Guard]] to collect [[The Mindrend Crown]] and place it in [[Sealed Vault]]. This however, was leaked (by [[Jessie Faux]]) to [[Ne're Darke]] as they sent a massive squadron to attack the patrol. The battle was long and ardious with many casualties initially and leading to a stalemale over an hour in. The main carriage in which the lockbox for [[The Mindrend Crown]] was held was guarded by [[Ardwin Hammer]], [[Jessie Faux]] and two other [[Sovereign Guard]], one of which was a [[Ne're Darke]] spy in disguise. They opened the carriage door and allowed a scrum to get inside in which the lockbox almost traded hands, but [[Jessie Faux]] was able to reclaim the box from the hands of [[Drax & Brax Blarglesnark]], in the process being badly wounded. 

Meanwhile, at around 12pm, so just as this battle is concluding, [[Ember's Creed]] and more specifically [[Eternal Flame]] staged a massive explosion. They detonated and estimated 15 barrels of Smokepowder within the Sewers next to [[Smokepowder Keep]], blasting a hole through the ground and into the [[Sealed Vault]]. From there, their numbers overwhelmed the 4 [[Sovereign Guard]] left to guard the vault, as everyone else was either guarding the city or on the patrol. It was a quick and relatively easy operation for the [[Eternal Flame]] excluding a narrow escape from [[Hannah Baker]] and her few recruits and they made out with [[The Ashen Horn]].

Also meanwhile, though technically a bit earlier, [[Factions/Aurora College|Aurora College]] along with [[The Magistrate]] planned an attack on [[The Royale Flush]] at [[Fortune's Favor]]. [[The Menagerie]] planned a heist, with [[Roach]] seeing [[Victory Day]] as the best time to strike, and not wanting to wait for [[Locations/Hideouts/Aurora College|Aurora College]] to get their hands on [[People/Precursors/The Sage Mind|The Sage Mind]]. So, [[The Menagerie]] sends [[Chang Ling]], disguised as [[Lady Luck]] to try to find their leader. [[Cassandro Blanc]] and [[Pyro]] wait atop the roof for a signal. [[Jynx]] walks through [[Fortune's Favor]] and causes havoc all around her, diverting attention. Meanwhile, [[Locations/Hideouts/Aurora College|Aurora College]] sends out their wizards and [[Weaveseers]] to attack the casino, sending a squad busting through the front doors and storming into the back. A battle ensues between them and [[The Royale Flush]], consisting of [[Royale Flush Jester]]s, [[Visi Parks]], [[Roger Parks]] and [[Quinn Hammer]]. [[The Menagerie]] sees this as the perfect diversion, and [[Chang Ling]] slinks into the back room. 

Unfortunately for them, [[The Maestro]] has already activated [[The Sage Mind]], and had seen this whole plan coming. He and [[Lady Luck]] sit patiently in his office, and as [[Chang Ling]] walks in, he is captured and fed to the [[Card Shark]]s. Hearing no word from him, [[The Menagerie]] assumes the worst and retreats. [[Factions/Aurora College|Aurora College]] brought plenty of reinforcements, but unfortunately for them, so did [[The Maestro]]. With a loud boom, entirety of [[Fortune's Favor]] is upended, tables flip, cards sent flying, and glasses shattering everywhere. 

Through this whole ordeal, [[Society of Higher Minds]] learns about the trading of these artifacts, and becomes particularly interested in [[The Sage Mind]] (and to a lesser extent [[The Ashen Horn]]).
