This is the final quest of act 1 and not really a full quest.

During [[Extra Eyes]] or just [[Victory Day]] in general, the party will have the opportunity to encounter / hear about all of the parallel events that happen today.

First, here is a timeline of what happens.

The city is all decorated up for Victory Day with candles along window sills, flowers strung over the streets, and large wreaths upon doors. The streets are bustling and filled with excitement and clamor as people exchange gifts and small treats. 

The festivities begin just before dawn, where a small group of the more religious type (mainly [[Ember's Creed]]) conduct their first pilgrimage by lantern light as they climb [[Searclaw's Rest]] in time for the sunrise. There is a large ceremony there known as the Dawnbreak, in which everyone gives thanks and offerings to Searclaw. The ceremony concludes as [[Searclaw]] emerges from his lair and takes to the skies, where he remains for the rest of the day. 

This year, those who participated in Dawnbreak get to use the Drakegliders created by [[Society of Higher Minds]] to glide back down into the city. 

The first part of the day is dedicated to celebration, as people will walk down the streets, hand out gifts and treats, and play music. 

Things to do:
Canal Rides through [[The Vive]]
Create Floating Lanterns
Food Installations
Band Performances (Stage 1 at the Docks, Stage 2 in Grand Plaza, Stage 3 in High Grounds)

At noon, the Lord of Hallowcrest, [[Elowyn Hallowbrand]] will come to Stage 3 for a speech in thanks to Searclaw and in memory of those who fought for the city.
