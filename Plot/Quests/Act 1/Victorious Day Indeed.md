This is the final quest of act 1 and not really a full quest.

During [[Extra Eyes]] or just [[Victory Day]] in general, the party will have the opportunity to encounter / hear about all of the parallel events that happen today.

First, here is a timeline of what happens.

The city is all decorated up for Victory Day with candles along window sills, flowers strung over the streets, and large wreaths upon doors. The streets are bustling and filled with excitement and clamor as people exchange gifts and small treats. 

The festivities begin just before dawn, where a small group of the more religious type (mainly [[Ember's Creed]]) conduct their first pilgrimage by lantern light as they climb [[Searclaw's Rest]] in time for the sunrise. There is a large ceremony there known as the Dawnbreak, in which everyone gives thanks and offerings to Searclaw. The ceremony concludes as [[Searclaw]] emerges from his lair and takes to the skies, where he remains for the rest of the day. 

This year, those who participated in Dawnbreak get to use the Drakegliders created by [[Society of Higher Minds]] to glide back down into the city. 

The first part of the day is dedicated to celebration, as people will walk down the streets, hand out gifts and treats, and play music.

Band Performances (Stage 1 at the Docks, Stage 2 in Grand Plaza, Stage 3 in High Grounds, Stage 4 at the Auditorium)

At noon, the Lord of Hallowcrest, [[Elowyn Hallowbrand]] will come to Stage 3 for a speech in thanks to Searclaw and in memory of those who fought for the city.

"Citizens of Hallowcrest, thank you for gathering here today. I hope you have enjoyed the festivities we and our lovely vendors and merchants have set up for you today. The day is just getting started, but before we continue with our festivities, I would like to remind you all what we are celebrating. Today is Victory Day. We call it that because it is the day in which we took back control from our oppressors. We concurred evil. We stood for justice and we triumphed. Our great protector Searclaw played a large part in that. And as he circles the sky above us now, then too he protected us from great threats." He pauses to allow the crowd to erupt into applause. Searclaw lets out an exhaling of flame in the sky in response.

"Let us also remember those who we lost. The brave and heroic members of the Sovereign Guard who fought for our freedoms and are sorely missed. Their sacrifice is what made this city. If you please, a moment of silence for my father, Gestalt Hallowbrand, and the thousands of other Sovereign Guard that we lost, as well as those who are still with us today."

As the moment of silence sits, a large explosion is heard in the background and a thick black cloud of smoke can be seen arising from [[Smokepowder Keep]].

The silence lingers for a few moments, people staring in fear, a sinking feeling covering the crowd. A few murmurs break out and then suddenly the entire crowd is discussing what has happened.
