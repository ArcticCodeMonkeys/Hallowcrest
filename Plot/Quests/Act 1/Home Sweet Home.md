
The party has been given a burned down tavern by [[Sir Finley Steinhart]] as reward for saving [[Dominic Hart]] during [[A Slightly Harder Quest]]. Upon arriving for the first time, they the place run down and completely destroyed. The windows are shattered, the walls scorched, ash and debris covering the floor, and a large wooden structural beam fallen from the ceiling splitting the bar in half. A black cat sits on the counter, staring at you as you enter. 

This black cat is actually an ex adventurer, transformed into a cat by [[Enya]] because she called her ugly. She retains most of her sentience as a cat and decided to live here since she was a regular at the old [[Fellowship of the Frontier]]. 

This quest is expected to be ongoing and never really resolved, but encapsulates the ongoing goal to improve their "home base".

We can use the Bastion System with the following changes:

- Bastion Turns are once a Tenday and conducted outside of sessions. If you fail to announce the Bastion turn for that Tenday, you do not take an action for the Bastion
- The Bastion is owned by the party, not one character. Orders are issued by the party as a collective. Two orders can be issued per tenday instead of 1.
- Each Special Facility must be built upon an existing Basic Facility. The Basic Facility must be of a sufficient size for the operation.
- The building already has 1 vast Facility, 1 Roomy Facility, and 4 cramped Facilitates. But they must be repaired first. Repairing them costs 1/4 of the build cost and repairing the Vast Facility turns it into the Pub Special Facility.
