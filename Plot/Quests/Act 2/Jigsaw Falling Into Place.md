[[The Maestro]] has unlocked the power of [[People/Precursors/The Sage Mind|The Sage Mind]], and while still learning to interpret its visions, he will soon become prescient. 

Though I won't write down his master plan, he has one. Thanks to the power of [[People/Precursors/The Sage Mind|The Sage Mind]], I can use retconning to display his precognition.

The Sage Mind has a limiting factor and is draining, so he can't always use it, but he can periodically create a plan using it.

His goal is to expand the [[The Royale Flush]]'s influence to monopolize the entire expansion of the [[Silver Coast]].

His current plan is to deal with [[The Menagerie]] and place blame on [[Ne're Darke]]. He'll then side with [[Sovereign Concord]] to take down [[Ne're Darke]] and use this to gain a great deal of influence. [[The Royale Flush]] will be an investment firm able to fund all the expansion to the new cities being built, provided of course, they are given certain immunities and laws are bent in their favour.

[[The Royale Flush]] is planning a strike [[The Other Bar]], where they will use a poisonous hallucinatory gas to take out the members. He suspects Roach will be the only one willing to take revenge, and will go to the [[Sovereign Guard]] to do so, he will be one step ahead. [[Roach]] will be thrown under the bus as an informant with fabricated evidence he was secretly a [[Ne're Darke]] agent. [[The Menagerie]] is tidied up and [[Ne're Darke]] is a target.
