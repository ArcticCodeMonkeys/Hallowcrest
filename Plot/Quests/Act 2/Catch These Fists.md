
This is the brawling tournament in [[Black and Blue]].

I'm thinking a card game style mini game.

Each fighter builds a deck, which must contain:

6 x Jab (Deal STR Score damage) [Action]
2 x Block (Block for CON Mod) [Reaction]
2 x Dodge (Block for DEX mod)  [Reaction]
2 x Deep Breath (Draw 3)  [Action]

And 6 more of Jab, Block, or Dodge in any Combination.
Finally, each fighter gets custom cards based of their Base Class which can help them out.

Play is simple, roll intimadation, initative, or performance to determine who goes first.
Everyone starts with 3 cards of their choice. At least one must be an Action card.

On your turn, you draw a card and play one  [Action] or [Power] card (unless you don't have one, in which case you skip your turn).

In response to an Action card, a player can play a Reaction card, often to reduce damage.

At the end of every 2 Rounds, the audience gets to play one card for each fighter.

## Audience Cards
Encourage (Target gets +CHA mod to next attack)  [Audience]
Insult" (Target cannot react for 1 round)  [Audience]

*Certain audience members have special cards they can play:*

Bless (+1d4 to a fighter's STR, CON, or DEX score) [Audience]
Spot Weakness (Target discards 2 cards of their choice)


## Special Cards:
Flurry of Blows (Deal Damage = STR Mod three times) [Action]
Powerful Punches (Whenever you deal unblocked damage, deal additional damage equal to your STR Mod) [Action]
Critical Strike (Whenever you deal damage, roll a d6. On a d6, the damage is doubled) [Power]
Inky Escape (Reduce all damage. Next attack has +DEX mod and reduce DEX mod from next attack taken)  [Reaction]
Rage (Whenever you take damage, reduce it by half CON mod) [Power]
Vampiric Hunger (Deal CON Score damage, regain HP equal to unblocked damage) [Action]
Dazing Blow (Deal STR Score damage, target shuffles 3 Dazed)
Acidic Spittle (Deal DEX score damage, cannot be reduced, and the target discards 2 cards of their choice) [[Action]]
Flaming Hook (Deal STR Score damage, target makes DC 16 Con Save at the start of each of their turns or takes STR mod damage and shuffles a Burn. Success ends the condition) [Action]
Park 'Em (Deal 2 X STR Score damage, target cannot react for 1 round)
