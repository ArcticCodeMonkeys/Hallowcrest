[[The Menagerie]] wants revenge for [[The Royale Flush]] killing [[Chang Ling]]. They are planning to send them a message and hit them where it hurts, their pockets. 

The [[Fortune's Favor]] chips are created in their secretive "Chip Shipper", a very expensive bit of artifice which turns one chip into many, stamping each one with a unique identifying number. Its designed to only work on a specific "Grandfather Chip", a plain chip that sits within the machine while working. When its not, it gets put in the vault. In that transition is where we can swap it for a dummy. A night, the Chip Shipper isnt guarded, so we sneak in, power it up, and pour out 50k worth of chips. Last bit is to launder it. 

Roach doesn't trust his crew to not get noticed after the last botched heist, so he asks the party to do it for him, and suggests they take at most one of his men.

- Need to take money
- Have option to screw over Royal Flush more, draws more suspicion (+Menagerie, -Royal Flush)
- Have option to get something for themselves, costs the mission a little bit (+item/money, -Menagerie)
- Can find a hidden path to [[The Maestro]]'s office (Very high risk, very high reward)

As a reward, they get a decent cut (30%), Renown, and some intel for [[Ashes to Ashes]].
