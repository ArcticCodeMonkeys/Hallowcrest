The [[Fortune's Favor]] chips are created in their secretive "Chip Shipper", a very expensive bit of artifice which turns one chip into many, stamping each one with a unique identifying number. Its designed to only work on a specific "Grandfather Chip", a plain chip that sits within the machine while working. When its not, it gets put in the vault. In that transition is where we can swap it for a dummy. A night, the Chip Shipper isnt guarded, so we sneak in, power it up, and pour out 50k worth of chips. Last bit is to launder it. 

Roach doesn't trust his crew to not get noticed after the last botched heist, so he asks the party to do it for him, and suggests they take at most one of his men.

# Heist Mechanics

# Scout The Joint

The party can wander and investigate all they want during the day while they prepare for the heist. They have a Heat meter, which indicates how suspicious they are being. Participating in the casino through gambling and drinking lowers the Heat by 1 each, and every suspicious activity increases the Heat by 1. Once they reach 3 Heat, they are escorted off the premises and temporarily banned. If they are caught in a restricted area or stealing something, they immediately reach 3 Heat.

While scouting, instead of perception or investigation checks, roll more specific checks related to the question itself. For example:
How thick does that door seem? (Roll Strength (Athletics))
How often do the guards seem to rotate? (Roll Wisdom (Insight))
Could we climb through those vents? (Roll Dexterity (Acrobatics))

# Make a Plan

The party can then return to Roach and make a plan. Roach will give them information based on a budget system, basically dictating what exactly Roach was able to discover during his research. They gain 20 Intel, which they can spend on the following pieces of information

Vents (5)
Jesters (5)
The Vault (5)
The Foundry (5)
The Boss (5)
Any specific room (1)
Any specific person (2)
Any specific event (2)
Any general rumor (3)

If they are not aware of any rooms or events, they can attempt to ask. If the room exists, they learn about it, if not, their intel is lost.

# Look the Part

The party needs to grab some gear, including some new fits. Formal attire will lower the attention they get while maneuvering around (5gp each). They can also purchase magic items and tools, Roach will give them Gossiper's Cuffs, which allow them to communicate to each other while within 30 feet of each other. 

Roach will also provide them with a Minor Charm of Dispelling (Able to dispel 1st level spells such as alarms, 3/day) and lend them his Watch of Planning (HE WANTS THIS BACK).

# Pull It Off:

Then comes the execution, the party has the same heat system as before, but with the added pressure that they need to get the heist done before they perform the routine check of the vault at midnight and shut off the machine. Every 10 minutes in real time, roll on the complications table.


## All Part of the Plan

Once per heist, each player can trigger a flashback. Turns out, they had prepared for this very situation. The character bought an item or casted a spell / ability which works perfectly in this scenario to resolve the issue. The character must still expend the resource (slot, gp, etc), but gets the effect immediately. Additionally, whatever effect it is automatically succeeds.

## Make Your Escape

There are multiple exits from Fortune's Favor, not all of them are equally accessible. The front foyer C1 will be the easiest to exit from in a stealthy escape where the alarm was never raised, however it will be one of the most guarded places in the event of an alarm. The Loading Dock is another option, much more difficult to get to and requiring taking out a few guards, but no one will suspect it when the alarm is tripped. The best place to leave from is the hardest place to get to. The Maestro's Office has a large pool which leads into the ocean, equipped with his submarine the Tritone which can be stolen and driven out. No-one will notice except the Maestro, who will have to be distracted to reasonably use this exit.


## Lose the Heat

If the party made a daring escape, they will have to contend with the five members of [[The Royale Flush]] which will be sent after them. The Jesters cannot leave [[Fortune's Favor]], but these members can. They must make an escape plan and plot a route through the city. Depending on the skill of the route, they may need to make an Acrobatics, Athletics, or Constitution check to ensure they can make the escape. The harder the route, the higher the DC is for [[The Royale Flush]] to follow them (Survival Check). Splitting up increases this DC further, but obviously makes the ensuing fight much harder (1v1).

# Features:


## Vents

The vents lead throughout the casino, including into the vault and chip foundry. Unfortunately, they are very small (tiny), and have numerous alarm spells on their entrance and exits. 

## Jesters

The Jesters are all connected to the Maestro's surveillance system, observable from his office (though he doesn't actively monitor it unless an alarm is triggered), C10 and C18. The Jesters are visibly quite powerful, likely being a difficult encounter for one on one. Fighting them all is not an option, especially evident since they fought off the [[The Menagerie]], [[The Magistrate]], and [[Factions/Aurora College|Aurora College]] all at once.

## The Vault

The vault stores their gold and chips, estimated to have anywhere from 15 to 50 thousand in gold and chips at a time. The vault is heavily sealed through two entrances. Both are a 3 foot thick adamantine door with a magical lock. A knock spell or dispel magic can disable the lock, but an alarm will trigger unless the passphrase is said. Only one person knows the passphrase, and who and what it is changes every day. For security measures, it is ensured that whoever is running the front desk is not the person with the passphrase. There is a very small (tiny creatures must squeeze) window between the vault and the foundry to pass chips through.

## The Foundry

The Foundry is home to the Chip Shipper, its only entrance is a thick metal door. The Cashier for the day ([[Beck Soleil]]) has the key. The Chip Shipper is an incredibly expensive piece of Higher Mind artifice. Powering it up may be noisy, so you'll need something to mask the sound. Should only take 10-15 minutes to print enough chips for this job to be worth it. The plan is simple, get in this room with the Master Chip, place your (or a member of [[The Menagerie]]'s) finger print on the chip, and run the machine. It should make a hefty chunk of change. Getting the chips out may prove difficult simply due to their sheer quantity, but I'm sure you'll be able to handle this.
