[[Grumsh Torval]]'s pigs were killed in the night. There is a clear sign of destruction as a large section of the fence has been toppled. He is unsure if whatever did this will come back, but was looking for someone to take watch over night for it.

Each night, he will pay 100 gold to the party for watching. Roll a d100, on a 70 or higher, the monster arrives, otherwise, decrease the DC by 10.

As the party stands guard besides the fence, they can see a shifting shadow in the distance hard to make out in the darkness of the night. 

Perception Check:
DC 10: It seems to be quite large, moving cautiously like a stalking predator
DC 13: You see the horns of a goat glisten in the moonlight.
DC 16: You can hear the low yet powerful growl of a lion and make out its flourishing mane
DC 20: You smell the distinct scent of sulfur and spot the faintest glimmer of flame in the eye of a dragon.

The creature stalks forward, if it sees any movement it pounces. If the party lets it get closer, they will see 6 Chimera Cubs follow up behind it in the hunt. If they act later, they arrive on the second round. 

The Chimera lives in the forest nearby with its cubs and was eating the pigs for survival.

Afterwards, [[Dr. Edward Monroe]] will arrive here to analyze the monstrosity under his current investigations. [[Grumsh Torval]] will tell him that the party was here fighting against it, and he sees a good opportunity for two things:

Seeing as they are so strong, he'd like them to continue finding and killing or even better, capturing, monstrosities as they find them. He offers 250 gold for every monstrosity they bring him that is dead, and 1000 for every one they bring alive. He wants to study them to try to find where they are coming from. 

He also sees that they are very strong while not really being affiliated, making them the perfect target for [[Panambre Stark]]'s zombies. He'll send a Patchwork to test their skill and will spectate from a distance.
