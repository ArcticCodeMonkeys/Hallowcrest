
A treasure map obtained by [[Sir Finley Steinhart]] and given out during [[Tournament of Champions]] leads to a dungeon previously inhabited by [[Precursors]]

- This dungeon will explain the lore of [[The Sage Mind]]
- Has some powerful magic items
- Will explain a bit of lore about [[Precursors]], as well as [[X]] and [[Y]].
- At the end, Awaken a precursor, upset that its bretheren have left it, and that humanoids are torturing its brother (Sage Mind), it will bring the dungeon crumbling down, leading to an escape scene
