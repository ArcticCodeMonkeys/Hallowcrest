An elf born in the Golden Fields, 'Mel' would grow to become a Cleric of Sune (the lady of love). Mel would meet Alexandria while the knight-errant was passing through the formers parish, tending to the Knights wounds. Alexandria would continue to return to that specific Parish for healing for the next 2 years, refusing help aside from the High-Elf. The two would eventually become partners and wed, taking each others last name (Vita from Mel & Gladius from Alex). Alex was eventually forced to settle down from her adventures due to age and injury catching up. The couple would have a total of 60 years together before Alexandria passed in her sleep. Alex's biggest regret was never convincing the Elf to go on an adventure with her, so she gave Mel her blade in the will, and bid her to continue Alexandria's adventure. 30 years on, Mel finally gets the opportunity to do this in the campaign, signing up to the transport job.
