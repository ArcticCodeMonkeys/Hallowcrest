
Felicity was born a chosen of Yeenoghu, filled with an insatiable hunger to feed Yeenoghu and gain him power. She constantly saw herself as many others did, a monster, while her parents tried to reassure her. Eventually, she fully transformed into a gnoll-like monster, running away and deciding to leave to hallowcrest both for answers, and to not harm her parents, who were heartbroken that she left.

She knows what she transformed into, but not about the connection to Yeenoghu, who is a lesser known being.
