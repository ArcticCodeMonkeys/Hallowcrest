
Here is what we want to invoke from each "villain" and then how we will do this:

 [[The Sculptor]] - Morbid Curiosity, Dedication to Science and Perfection. Cold and Analytical. 
 
 We will try to invoke the feeling that he is greatly flawed and evil but is wasting his talents on that. We do this by showing that his inventions as [[Dr. Edward Monroe]] will be genuinely helpful and impactful. They will heal people, cure diseases, act as prosthetics, etc. If he wasn't so invested in making the perfect life form, he would be a marvel in medicine.
