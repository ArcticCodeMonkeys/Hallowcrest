


Who:
A Nobleman, rich and large investor to the Merchant's Guild. Had a small but growing following of political supporters who criticized [[Elowyn Hallowbrand]] for his youth and inexperience as a leader.

What:
He was killed, not witnessed but heard by a passerby on the street. Just a minute later as they came to investigate, they saw a large pool of blood on the ground outside the door of his house which was left wide open. The body was missing, but a trail of blood lead through the house, into the back alley, and towards a sewer grate before disappearing. 


Where:
Outside the nobleman's house

How:

The method of killing is unclear as the body is missing, but clearly some sort of physical trauma due to the large amount of blood.

When:
Very early morning (4am)


Key Suspects:

The Wife:

- The Wife was not at home during the time of the murder, she appeared almost an hour later after investigators had already arrived at the scene.
- She was taken in for questioning
- Possible motive: Her husband was cheating?

Elowyn Hallowbrand or one of his supporters:

- The Nobleman was an avid critic of Hallowbrand and perhaps he or someone close to him saw him as a threat
- This lead is not very solid

A Merchant or Guild Member:

-  The Nobleman's ledger will show large sums of money being transfered between over 30 different guilds and organizations, some of which are just mentioned as shorthands such as "Support (2000gp), N.D" and "Supplies (500gp), M"
- Could be some act of jealousy or retribution for a deal gone wrong

Key Detail:

The body is missing and the murder occured at a specific time (just as he was leaving / entering his house), so this murder was likely pre-meditated. The fact that the body is missing also indicates that the killer is either very careful and calculated, or would be an obvious suspect and felt the need to hide the evidence.

Answer:
The Nobleman is having an affair. This is a bit of an open secret amongst the merchant guild. He goes out every Friday nights to the local club for the Half Price Ale and Room deal, and has an affair with one of the workers. As a merchant himself, the Sculptor knows this, and so sets up a pulley system in a nearby sewer entrance in the back alley behind the house, waits outside his house for the Nobleman to come back home from his affair, and kills him as he is opening the door. He drags his body through the house, doing so quickly to avoid being spotted, and into the back alley. He places the body on the pulley system and uses a preplaced cart to wheel the body through the sewers and back to his lair. He targeted the nobleman due to his many connections (good for other suspects), his physical weakness (to see the enhancing qualities of elixir and necromancy), and his awareness of his schedule (requiring no stalking). Panambre Stark is the one who conducts the necromancy, working with The Sculptor as his apprentice (among others).
