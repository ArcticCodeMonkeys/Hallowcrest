
This is the main storyline for the campaign, revolving around the three artifacts in [[Hallowcrest]]. 

- [[The Ashen Horn]] is currently located in the [[Sealed Vault]], in possession of the [[Sovereign Concord]].
- [[The Mindrend Crown]] is currently lost, but [[Ne're Darke]] is looking for it again.
- [[Items/Artifacts/The Sage Mind|The Sage Mind]] is currently being brought into [[Hallowcrest]] by [[The Party]], to be handed over to [[Factions/Aurora College|Aurora College]].

Act 1 should have the party either successfully bring [[Items/Artifacts/The Sage Mind|The Sage Mind]] to [[Locations/Hideouts/Aurora College|Aurora College]], or have it stolen from them by [[The Royale Flush]]. This storyline will kinda fall away for most of act 1, as they will spend a majority of their time getting set up in the city. Levels: 3-5

Act 2 will have the party be flung into the middle of a conflict, as [[Ne're Darke]] finds [[The Mindrend Crown]]. Working for a faction or their own interests, they will seek to gain money and renown while uncovering the secret Grand Game going on throughout the city. The end of Act 2 is marked by the party either heisting or acquiring one of the artifacts, either on behalf of themselves or a faction. Levels: 5-7

Act 3 will plunge the party further into the conflict, as they are now a key player in the Grand Game. Many factions will both attempt to ally themselves and steal the artifact from them, and the party can choose who to fully side with and lead to victory. Most factions will push them to continue the Grand Game and acquire all three artifacts to safeguard Hallowcrest's future to them. Acquiring the second artifact marks the end of act 3. Levels: 8-10

Act 4 will involve planning a heists for the last artifacts as well as defending their two artifacts. It is likely that one of their artifacts will be stolen, and this arc could potentially be the longest. It is only ended by one faction (or the party) acquiring all three artifacts. Levels: 10-12

Act 5 is the final conflict of the campaign. The party will either need to defend against a full scale seige, force out an opposing faction, or attempt to heist the three artifacts if they dont have them, in order to decide the fate of hallowcrest. Level: 12
