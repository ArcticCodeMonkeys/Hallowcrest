# Hallowcrest

Hallowcrest is an Obsidian campaign wiki for a fantasy tabletop roleplaying campaign. It collects the setting's factions, locations, people, items, history, world-building concepts, and plot material in one linked vault.

## Start Here

- Open [`index.md`](index.md) for the public-facing wiki entry point.
- Browse the folders below for focused setting information.
- Follow `[[Wiki Links]]` between related notes to move through the setting.
- Check [`Admin/TODO.md`](Admin/TODO.md) for ongoing world-building and campaign-planning work.

## Vault Structure

- `Admin/` - planning notes, motifs, and editorial tasks.
- `Concepts/` - setting systems and ideas such as magic, religion, currency, and festivals.
- `Factions/` - major powers and organizations in Hallowcrest.
- `Guilds and Organizations/` - guilds, teams, houses, and public services.
- `Historical Events/` - the timeline and important events from the setting's past.
- `Items/` - artifacts, equipment, and faction-associated objects.
- `Locations/` - cities, districts, stores, taverns, hideouts, and other points of interest.
- `People/` - NPCs and other named characters, grouped by affiliation or role.
- `Plot/` - quests, villains, player plots, session notes, and campaign arcs.
- `Images/` - visual assets organized by subject.
- `_templates/` - Obsidian templates and vault utilities.

## Campaign Direction

The campaign is centered on the Grand Game: a growing conflict among Hallowcrest's factions involving alliances, heists, ancient artifacts, and the future of the city. The planned story progresses through four acts, moving from exploration and faction ties to investigation, artifact heists, and a final struggle that determines Hallowcrest's fate.

## Working In This Vault

This project is intended to be edited as an Obsidian vault. Keep notes in the folder that best matches their subject, use internal links for names and related topics, and place images in the corresponding folder under `Images/`. The `publish` frontmatter field is used by the wiki's publishing workflow; preserve it when editing notes that are meant to appear on the published site.
