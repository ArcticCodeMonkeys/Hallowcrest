
There are 7 main factions in Hallowcrest:

- [[Ember's Creed]] - Religious organization, monks, paladins, clerics and warlocks devout to Searclaw
- [[Ne're Darke]] - Criminal organization, organized crime, smuggling, drug dealing and assasinations
- [[Society of Higher Minds]] - Elite organization, artificers and wizards dedicated to invention and discovery
- [[Sovereign Concord]] - Law enforcement organization, guards employed by the Capital to protect Hallowcrest
- [[The Keepers of Oak]] - Spiritual organization, druids and rangers devoted to protecting nature and wildlife
- [[The Menagerie]] - Criminal organization, misfits and outcasts dedicated to drug dealing and thievery
- [[The Royale Flush]] - Criminal organization, gamblers and bards commiting extortion, torture, espionage and tax evasion


Joining a faction:

Each faction has specific requirements to join them. If you get into a faction, they will give you token to indicate that you are a member. 


Renown:

Renown is a system which indicates how well liked you are by a certain faction. Renown can be used to gain access to faction services, favors, and resources. Renown can be gained from completing quests, favors, or taking political actions which favor the faction.
