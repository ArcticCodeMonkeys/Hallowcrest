In 678 DE, all of the major civilizations in the plane came together to form a peace alliance, excluding Ne're Darke. Signing a treaty ensuring that they would never break this peace, the civilizations estabilized the Sovereign Concord, a global alliance in peace, trade, and strength, and establising the Soverign Era (SE). By 127 SE, the Capital was constructed, the largest city in the Plane where all trades went through and the rich and elite were able to live burdenless lives. 

In 1274 SE, the Soverign Concord heard word of Ne're Darke plotting something in the far and recently discovered Silver Coast. After sending some scouts, they learned that Ne're Darke plotted to take control of a great dragon, by the name of Daurgothoth, using psionic crystals located deep underground. Fearing a hostile takeover, the Soverign Concord sent an army to combat Ne're Darke and stop them from gaining control over Daurgothoth, lead by the great general Gestalt Hallowbrand. In the Battle of Ash and Ember, Gestalt and his army successfully defeated Ne're Darke's forces, though resulting in his untimely demise. 

Upon the establishment of Hallowcrest in 1276 SE, the Soverign Concord sent a large platoon of forces to build and defend the city, as well as a large amount of tradesmen, nobles, and adventurers from the Capital to inhabit the city and capitalize on its rich resources and undiscovered land. 

### Presence in Hallowcrest

In Hallowcrest, the Sovereign Concord is the presiding force controlling the city, creating laws, and enforcing justice. Lead by the lord of Hallowcrest, Gestalt Stellaris, the Sovereign Concord has a large group of trained city guards, called the Sovereign Guard.
